# Trial commercial terms in Pricing — contract proposal

**Additional scope reduction — user decision, 2026-10-15.** Defer trial extension and repeat trials, standalone acquisition without Orders, scheduled composition changes and scheduled resume, linked predecessor/successor subscription replacement, and changes of an existing subscription's base plan. Reject these operations before effects, including through operator/direct wrappers; earlier procedures and decisions for them are future traceability only. New paid and trial subscriptions originate through Orders. Existing-subscription lifecycle operations, supported immediate quantity increases/component additions, future order starts, cancellation scheduling, renewal and normal trial timers remain. Technical retry/successor attempts for the same acquisition remain recovery, not linked replacement of an existing subscription.

Free-period and credit trials, normal expiry/conversion and early conversion to the already accepted paid target remain in scope with their Pricing/Billing/financial gates. Trial-to-paid conversion is not a general changePlan capability. Preserve trial history/eligibility evidence and refuse repeat requests for the same payer/plan, including via a new subscription; a transport retry is not a repeat trial. No extension or repeat becomes available from a Pricing policy alone. Independent cancellation and new purchase have separate resource/data lifecycles: there is no automatic resource adoption, data transfer, place handover or continuity promise. Cancellation retains its existing retention/disposition rules; it does not mean immediate deletion. Unsupported plan changes cannot silently become cancel+new.

**Dependency confirmation — 2026-10-15:** the user confirmed a separate Pricing extension for the optional PlanRevision trial terms and a separate Billing promotional-grant contract for credit trials. Current Pricing V1 does not supply these target capabilities. This confirms the ownership and delivery boundary below; it does not enable trials before their applicable contracts are delivered, add trial fields to Products or approve any further scope deferral.

**Reciprocal package — 2026-10-15:** [TP01-TP06](#10-reciprocal-trial-delivery-contract) now distinguishes verified provider baselines, required extensions and numbered joint scenarios; scoped Pricing, Orders, Rating and Ledger changes are recorded in the [component proposals](proposals/README.md), with their own documents unchanged. Billing provider delivery and exact SDK/accounting mappings remain open.

**Status — current scope, 2026-10-15:** semantic contract proposal for initial free-period and credit trials, normal/early paid conversion and shared Billing bonus accounting. Immutable PlanRevision/receipt, staged grant finalization, original expiry, eligible carryover and full-result correction/liability are specified below. Trial exceptions, standalone acquisition and tenant transfer are deferred. External owner ratification, SDK mapping, financial posting implementation and runtime evidence remain open; no Rust implementation is claimed. Earlier product decisions are retained in [DECISIONS](DECISIONS.md).

**Initial readiness compatibility amendment — 2026-10-18.** Initial free-period and credit trials are subject to [IA-2026-10-18](features/05-entitlements.md#initial-activation-readiness-contract): usable service readiness must precede the fresh local active/T commit (SUB-D-55). The staged credit startup below remains a proposed grant/accounting sequence, but its path that waits for post-T grant finalization before essential access is **not an enabled initial activation path**. If final grant evidence is necessary for usable access, the initial credit profile stays gated pending a jointly agreed Billing/entitlement coverage/readiness contract that breaks this dependency. Prepared coverage is still unissued/unspendable; neither this amendment nor a ready event invents pre-T credit, an actual T, another grant or a shifted expiry. Existing grant recovery/corrections, early conversion and ongoing accounting retain their rules. Exact resolution is an integration dependency, not presumed provider delivery.

## 1. Decision and baseline

Existing Pricing Plan/PlanRevision is the storefront offer. The storefront combines Products descriptions/SKU identity with the selected Pricing version. Pricing owns an optional trial commercial block on that revision; Subscriptions retains the customer acceptance and owns actual episode dates, expiry and conversion; extension is deferred. There is no approved need for independently reusable TrialOffer revisions across plans. Products stores no trial-policy fields; Entitlements materializes access.

| Variant | Pricing terms | Financial treatment | Ending condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free period | Explicit zero-price bindings for the specified trial scope and explicit duration semantics | Rating uses those accepted zero terms; missing money is not free pricing | Accepted duration/expiry policy |
| Trial credit | Ordinary price bindings unchanged; explicit bonus amount, currency, applicable products/items/charge kinds and duration | Rating values served trial facts at ordinary tariffs; one shared Billing grant covers eligible value through the agreed benefit/invoice contract | Accepted credit expiry or authoritative exhaustion policy |

A revision selects one variant; combining them is not approved. Trial bonus is not payment capture, prepaid customer funds, authorization to serve on credit, or a zero tariff. General promotions, paid-trial policy, recurring allowances and prepaid-wallet implementations remain outside this authorization. No example supplies a numeric default, credit limit or repeat-trial entitlement.

Current [Pricing PRD §4.2](../../pricing/docs/PRD.md#42-out-of-scope) still excludes trials/allowances/prepaid grants from the delivered baseline, and D-504/D-510 V1 support is unchanged. The PlanRevision trial block and shared trial-credit accounting are explicit target extensions; historical V1 refusal remains correct until the new capabilities are ratified and delivered.

| Concern | Accepted direction | Still proposed/open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer structure | Optional trial block on immutable PlanRevision, using existing `planId`/`revisionId` | Exact schema, publication/retirement ordering and supported bindings |
| Trial lifecycle | Subscriptions owns customer acceptance association and actual episode boundaries; Entitlements owns access | Owner interfaces, replay and completion evidence; extension is deferred |
| Trial credit | Billing owns the bounded bonus and shared allocation/correction; Pricing owns terms, Rating gross valuation, Ledger necessary postings under an agreed contract | §4 specifies proposed evidence/operations/atomic recovery; owner ratification, wire/schema, implementation and remaining policy/enforcement gates persist; corrected credit stays unavailable after original expiry |
| Paid conversion | Existing agreed prepay/postpay guards; failed prepay defaults to no paid start | Detailed due/dunning, retry/later paid association and runtime fixtures |
| Execution validity | Frozen policy is historical evidence, not unlimited authority | Applicable S-Q01 final permit and original future commitment before any advance capture |

## 2. Ownership

| Owner | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Pricing | Immutable PlanRevision trial terms: selected variant, duration semantics, price/bonus policy, paid target, applicability and policy references; authoritative commercial acceptance/fulfilment inputs; no mutable balance |
| Subscriptions | Accepted revision/receipt association, customer trial history and actual episode dates; guarded expiry/conversion, amount-less commercial facts and `CommercialPostureV1` publication to Entitlements; no raw metering, monetary valuation or remaining-balance calculation |
| Products | Descriptions, SKU identity/structure/versions and lifecycle/reference protection; no trial-policy fields |
| Product policy owners (people) / explicitly bound Contracts | Approve policy and permitted bounds; Contracts supplies explicitly referenced legal/commercial terms, not a separate universal trial catalog |
| Billing trial-benefit accounting | Accepted owner of promotional grant issuance/history, consumed/remaining balance, authoritative outcomes, allocation and correction; operational benefit records are separate from Pricing terms and Subscriptions lifecycle |
| Rating / Billing / Ledger | Rating supplies canonical versioned full valuations at frozen tariffs. Billing coordinates benefit allocation with its invoice accounting/corrections; Ledger performs necessary financial postings only under the separately agreed integration contract |
| Payments / designated credit-service owner | Payment outcomes and authorization for postpay service; neither is implied by a promotional trial-credit balance |
| Entitlements / platform enforcement / resource provider | Desired-state derivation from `CommercialPostureV1`, reconciliation, access materialization and point-of-use/resource enforcement with authoritative outcomes; their asynchronous behavior is separate from financial coverage |

Pricing owns commercial eligibility inputs; Subscriptions owns episode/history evidence. Trial extension and repeat trials are deferred even with Pricing policy permission. Preserve history and refuse repeat acquisition for the same payer/plan across subscription IDs; technical replay retains the original episode. Additional bonus, stacking, transfer, rollover, regrant and reactivation remain unavailable. Early guarded conversion retains eligible unused credit under its original expiry and applicability. Exact initial eligibility, evidence and Billing accounting contracts remain required. Earlier exception ceilings are future policy context, not current schema/implementation prerequisites.

The current [Ledger SDK credit application contract](../../ledger/ledger-sdk/src/api.rs) funds its reusable-credit grant from the payer's real settled unallocated cash, capped by that live pool, and applies it against open receivables. It is not a free promotional grant. Do not fabricate settled cash or treat `post_credit_application` as a delivered trial-bonus API. The proposed Billing benefit records and necessary Ledger posting integration require a separate agreed contract; no new journal, tax or recognition rules are selected here.

## 3. Proposed commercial evidence

This semantic shape describes the proposed contract, not final SDK field names, API versions or persistence layout. Existing plan/revision identities replace the earlier separate offer identity.

### 3.1 Reusable offer and immutable revision

| PlanRevision input | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and scope | Existing `planId`/`revisionId`, catalog/seller scope and publication provenance; exact SKU/plan-item/price/book/currency bindings |
| Optional trial block | Absent means no trial offer; present selects **free period OR credit**, never an implicit combined benefit |
| Duration | Explicit value, unit, start semantics and boundary-calculation policy; Subscriptions derives actual dates from its valid applied start, with no default duration |
| Free-period benefit | Explicit zero-price bindings and the products/items/intervals covered; unsupported or missing price evidence refuses |
| Credit benefit | Bonus amount and currency plus explicit eligible products/items and charge kinds; ordinary price bindings remain unchanged. No all-products or FX fallback |
| Paid target | Intended plan/item/price scope and agreed resolution/acceptance mechanism, distinguishing what is frozen from any separately accepted future resolution |
| Policy links | Versioned settlement, expiry/exhaustion, initial eligibility and repeat-refusal policy; legal terms only where explicitly bound |

Publication is immutable. Changing a duration, amount, applicability or paid target produces a new PlanRevision; accepted customer evidence is not silently rewritten. A change from fourteen to seven days illustrates this protection, not a default. Retirement may prevent new acceptance but cannot erase accepted history or a protected paid promise. Exact lifecycle ordering remains an owner contract.

### 3.2 Customer acceptance and subscription episode

The reusable policy on PlanRevision is distinct from a customer's accepted receipt, Subscriptions episode and, for the credit variant, individual grant. Acceptance binds the exact revision and commercial evidence to the authorized acquisition/customer scope, preserving frozen policy and applicability. A transport retry cannot create a second acceptance/episode/grant; changed attempts/orders remain subject to S-U01 association rules.

Subscriptions commits actual boundaries and retains immutable episode evidence; extensions are deferred. It consumes authoritative credit outcomes by reference/version; it does not copy a mutable ledger or issue monetary allocation facts. Receipt/grant identity is not an S-Q04 setup-charge occurrence or proof of a newly chargeable business action.

Historical evidence remains readable after publication/retirement, but does not authorize execution forever. Trial/paid-target acceptance must cover the intended operation and applicable final permit. Expired coverage requires supported fresh explicit acceptance or the protected-right remedy, never silent repricing or extending a V1 hold/permit.

### 3.3 Shared trial-credit accounting

S-U15 must establish Billing's shared promotional-benefit contract rather than a separate full bonus per product or subscription item; owner direction is accepted, delivery is not claimed. A customer grant has durable identity tied to the accepted acquisition/trial episode, immutable source PlanRevision/policy/applicability and the authorized payer/beneficiary scope. The proposed identity/scope and evidence contract is in §4; exact wire mapping still requires owner ratification.

The contract must support exactly-once issuance/replay, issued/consumed/remaining/expiry history, authoritative versioned balances/outcomes and recoverable lost responses. Consumption across eligible items draws from the same grant once. Currency and charge-kind eligibility must be explicit; a one-time charge still needs its separately agreed S-Q04 trigger and cannot become chargeable merely because a bonus could cover it.

Billing consumes canonical versioned **full Rating replacements**, retaining the previously accepted result/allocation and their lineage. It adjusts the same grant once under the original scope, currency and frozen terms. Replay of the same accepted version is a no-op; conflicting version/content refuses or enters explicit recovery, and stale results cannot restore credit again. This does not introduce independent financial delta delivery. Invoice coordination uses the established draft replacement or posted correction path and the required Ledger integration; no posted invoice is financially rewritten inline.

Illustrative same-currency case: grant 100, accepted consumption 30, remaining 70. Corrected usage at the **same accepted tariff** yields a full replacement value of 25; Billing adjusts the same allocation and restores 5, making 75 available while the grant remains valid and applicable to an active trial or eligible paid usage after early guarded conversion, until the same original expiry. If this correction arrives after grant expiry, historical net consumption becomes 25 and conceptual accounting remaining becomes 75, but spendable credit is 0. Billing preserves the original expiration; correction never extends the trial. These are semantic outcomes, not prescribed API fields or storage. Repeating that version restores nothing further. This is neither new grant/top-up, cash refund nor catalog repricing; the numbers are not policy defaults. If a trial already ended by exhaustion, a later correction cannot automatically reopen it even before original expiry. Illustratively, consumption of the full 100 corrected to 95 restores 5 in accounting, but grants no service/spend permission for the closed episode and does not reverse an applied paid conversion. This does not end a still-active trial or prevent valid/applicable same-grant restoration during it. V1 refuses reactivation of a closed episode, rollover past original expiry and regrant on the same subscription. A separate new acquisition may be a fresh paid purchase; repeat trials are deferred; its association/recovery contract remains gated. Correction alone authorizes none of these actions or a new setup-charge trigger. Eligible pre-boundary trial positive corrections follow the accepted platform-liability rule below; correction/liability cases outside that scope remain gated.

On early valid guarded trial-to-paid conversion, unused credit remains on the **same grant**, available toward eligible paid-plan expenses until the **same original expiration**, under unchanged currency, product, item, charge-kind and other accepted restrictions. Illustratively, a 100-credit/14-day grant with 60 remaining on day 5 keeps that 60 available after conversion only until original day 14; no new 100, new 14-day period, top-up or grant is issued. These are examples, not policy defaults. This conversion closes the trial episode without making its still-valid grant unspendable. Same-grant full Rating replacement/correction lineage applies to eligible paid usage at that usage's frozen agreed tariffs. A paid-plan SKU outside original grant applicability cannot spend this balance, but does not erase it; no broader product scope or FX is inferred. No expired grant is revived, trial access renewed or applied paid conversion reversed. The benefit does not by itself satisfy prescribed upfront payment unless the agreed coverage/settlement contract permits it; existing prepay/postpay guards remain. Stacking, transfer and additional bonus are unavailable even with Pricing permission; initial eligibility and supported owner contracts remain required. No automatic new-period rollover or regrant follows.

Keep these distinct: **served evidence → rated gross value → eligible bonus coverage / platform-borne amount / customer obligation**, with the successor paid-target boundary recorded separately. Credit trials produce legitimate served/usage facts before paid-target conversion, valued at ordinary rates. References to policy/grant/coverage evidence may accompany amount-less Subscriptions facts under the agreed contract; bonus amounts, balance and allocation math do not become Subscriptions fact fields. No-paid-start or expiry-no-debt rules must not erase trial facts needed for rating/drawdown.

For eligible accepted-trial expenses served before the authoritative applied paid boundary, the platform bears full rated value beyond bounded bonus coverage, including positive corrections, errors and accounting/stopping-delay or in-flight overrun; it creates no customer trial-overage debt. Usage actually served after a valid guarded paid transition follows accepted paid-plan/settlement terms after eligible bonus coverage. Classify by served time and the applied boundary, never rating/event/correction arrival; split crossing usage using supported evidence and never backdate paid conversion. Illustratively, remaining bonus 2 and eligible trial usage value 5 caused by that delay means bonus coverage 2 and platform-borne 3, with no customer debt for that 3. If the usage occurred after valid guarded paid conversion, the remaining 3 instead follows the agreed paid-plan/settlement terms. Late-reported pre-conversion usage keeps its trial classification after conversion. Rating preserves actual gross usage/value; Billing distinguishes bounded bonus allocation, platform-borne amount and customer obligation. The grant never becomes negative, receives an unlimited top-up or pretends to cover 5. An erroneous billed trial tail uses the existing idempotent Billing correction path; no refund method or new Ledger journal/tax schema is selected.

The subsequent 2026-10-08 approval also covers positive corrections and errors for eligible accepted-trial service before the authoritative applied paid boundary. Illustratively, a grant of 100 with prior gross consumption 90 and corrected full gross value 110 yields bonus coverage 100, platform cost 10 and customer obligation 0. Billing replaces the same source allocation once under original tariff/version lineage; gross remains 110. It does not create negative bonus, fabricated coverage/top-up, a new grant or cash refund. Any erroneous customer posting uses existing idempotent correction/remedy paths. This does not waive prior legitimate debt, explicit retention charges, excluded charge kinds or unrelated recovery/remedy obligations. Financial protection is separate from access enforcement: detect/enforce/recover contracts and supported evidence remain S-U15 launch gates, and other liability cases remain open. No unlimited free operation or instantaneous stop is promised. Unknown balance is neither zero/exhausted nor permission to spend.

Exhaustion/expiry follows authoritative versioned outcomes and the accepted effective-time policy. For an active unconverted trial, it initiates guarded trial end or an eligible paid conversion, never automatic paid permission. After early conversion has already applied, retained-grant expiry/exhaustion ends only bonus coverage; it does not itself stop or revoke paid service, duplicate conversion or reverse it. Remaining paid expenses follow the accepted paid-plan/settlement rules and applicable guards; no unsupported liability or waived payment prerequisite is inferred. Overage or excluded charges require their own agreed paid basis and applicable guards; there is no default pay-as-you-go fallback or zero-list-rate workaround. Early guarded conversion preserves eligible unused credit as above; stacking and additional bonus are refused, transfer is deferred, and v1 has no new-period rollover, regrant or reactivation route (accepted 2026-10-08); a new acquisition is a fresh purchase; repeat trials are deferred. A correction cannot automatically revive a closed episode, retrigger conversion or reverse an applied paid conversion. No blanket forgiveness, customer-funds refund or fixed balance policy is adopted.

## 4. Candidate provider capabilities

These describe proposed behavior, not shipped endpoint/type names, new grants or versions.

| Candidate capability | Required boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Exact PlanRevision trial read | Authorized immutable revision, selected variant and complete semantic inputs; no separate TrialOffer lookup/catalog requirement |
| Commercial eligibility and acceptance | Evaluate the published policy using authoritative permitted evidence; bind exact revision/terms to the acquisition and recover the same frozen receipt on replay |
| Operation-time fulfilment evidence | Check validity for the named trial/paid operation against lifecycle ordering; an observation is not the S-Q01 final permit |
| Shared trial-credit issue/read/outcomes | Proposed Billing S-U15 provider supplies durable grant identity, idempotent issuance and authorized versioned history/balance/expiry/exhaustion outcomes |
| Credit allocation and invoice coordination | Billing consumes canonical full Rating replacements and preserves prior allocation/result/version lineage; idempotent same-grant adjustment integrates with draft replacement or posted correction and agreed Ledger postings |

The following S-U15 semantic contract is a **reviewable technical proposal**, not a delivered API, final SDK/wire version, storage schema or external-owner ratification. Accepted product rules in §3.3 constrain it; remaining product policies cannot be filled in by an adapter. The capability names and fields below name meanings only. Exact receipt encoding, supported policy bounds, retention and capability negotiation still require owner agreement. Offer/price/SKU retirement races need authoritative fences, not detached reads. V1 continues to refuse unsupported trial/credit inputs.

**Proposed evidence envelope**

| Semantic field group | Meaning and authority |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and business association | `grant_id`; immutable benefit-issuance identity scoped to seller and accepted acquisition; an explicit acquisition-origin discriminator and accepted receipt. Orders acquisitions carry order/version/line; standalone acquisition is unavailable. Subscription/episode association is retained where established. Transport command and attempt identities are correlation, not permission to mint another grant. S-U01 governs any successor/rebinding |
| Frozen source | Exact PlanRevision and accepted policy/receipt references plus digests; issued amount, currency, eligible products/items/charge kinds and payer/beneficiary scope. Billing verifies the authorized Pricing evidence instead of accepting caller-invented money or silently reading today's catalog |
| Original validity | Preparation retains accepted duration/start semantics and intended episode, without inventing actual T or expiration. Finalization binds the authoritative applied T and derives original expiration from the same frozen policy. Historical policy is immutable; replay/correction never moves expiry. Prepared coverage is not issued/spendable credit; missing final evidence remains pending |
| Lifecycle evidence | Subscription/episode identity, authoritative lifecycle revision and applied trial/paid/end boundaries, including reason and retained-grant eligibility after early conversion. An observation timestamp is distinct from effective served time; payment/credit-service permissions retain their separate owners |
| Current accounting evidence | Authoritative grant revision, issued amount, accepted allocation totals, historical remaining and the policy/evidence controlling current eligibility. A positive historical remaining amount is not a service permission or unconditional spend authorization; expired grants remain unavailable |
| Valuation and prior application | Canonical Rating result identity/version/digest and full replacement reference; fact/version, frozen tariff/calculation evidence and served extents; prior accepted result/allocation version and digest. No independent monetary delta input; Subscriptions supplies amount-less boundary/attribution references, not raw usage or allocation math |
| Allocation and financial lineage | Allocation revision; per supported extent, gross value, bonus coverage, platform-borne amount and customer obligation, in the result's currency/precision; original grant scope plus served-time/applied-boundary evidence. Invoice/period identity and draft/posted correction or required Ledger-operation references record financial completion separately |
| Recovery metadata | Durable operation correlation, payload digest, accepted/current revision, explicit pending/refused/applied disposition, effective time versus recorded time, ordered outcome sequence and read barrier. Preserve prior applied financial evidence when a new result is staged but not yet applicable |

The breakdown is Billing's proposed allocation output, not new Subscriptions fact fields or Ledger journal accounts. For each fully classified supported charge extent, gross value is apportioned once among bonus coverage, platform-borne amount and customer obligation before separate invoice-level rounding/tax/other financial functions. Incomplete attribution, an unresolved policy case or missing paid-boundary evidence produces an unresolved allocation, never a guessed customer amount. Grant applicability remains unchanged; no FX, excluded-charge coverage or trial tariff applied to a different paid-plan fact is inferred.

**Proposed operations and response semantics**

| Operation | Request evidence | Durable response / refusal boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare or recover coverage | Authorized Orders or direct business-origin/benefit identity, command correlation/digest, exact accepted Pricing receipt, frozen scope/currency/amount/duration and intended episode | Return one durable prepared-coverage identity, designated consumer/association and reserved grant identity with revision/digest. This is coverage authorization for the staged protocol, not issued/spendable credit or actual T. Replay returns the same preparation; changed content conflicts. Unsupported origin or missing policy/evidence refuses or remains explicitly unresolved |
| Finalize the prepared grant | Same prepared/grant identity plus authoritative Subscriptions applied operation/revision, episode and actual S-Q01 T, with current cancellation/end evidence | Derive original expiry from that T and frozen duration, and issue the same grant once. Replay/lost response returns the same finalization; no new grant or fresh start. Late finalization preserves original expiry, even if already expired, and cannot authorize access or revive a closed episode |
| Settle an unapplied preparation | Same preparation and authoritative non-application/tombstone evidence fenced against local activation for the intended episode | Release/settle only the proven unapplied preparation; unknown activation is recovered, not assumed absent. An already applied T takes the finalization/history path, not an abort that erases it |
| Read grant / recover outcome | Authorized grant or business identity; required source/grant revision where applicable; prior operation identity or outcome cursor | Return an authoritative versioned snapshot, source references, eligibility reasons and separate allocation/financial-completion status. Explicit not-found is distinct from unavailable/unknown; neither proves zero balance or failed issuance. Lost issuance responses are recovered by the business identity, not a new grant request |
| Apply canonical full Rating replacement | Authorized canonical result reference/version/digest, expected prior result/allocation and grant revisions, complete frozen tariff/served-time/attribution evidence and applicable lifecycle/paid-boundary references | Return the new allocation/grant revisions and breakdown, or the stored same-version result. A newer result awaiting evidence/policy is visibly pending with its prior applied allocation preserved. Positive corrections outside eligible accepted-trial expenses served before the applied paid boundary return a policy gate rather than an invented debt/waiver |
| Recover ordered outcomes and financial work | Authorized grant/acquisition scope, cursor/barrier or durable financial-work identity | Return issue/allocation/exhaustion/expiry outcomes with authoritative revisions, effective/recorded times and gap evidence, plus recoverable invoice/Ledger disposition. Expiry/exhaustion is evidence for the guarded consumer, not direct access revocation or paid-transition authorization |

An issued grant cannot be claimed twice by changed attempts or rebuilt drafts. The provider proposes one durable benefit identity per accepted acquisition and selected grant policy; an accepted rebind changes association under a fenced S-U01 operation while preserving the original grant. An unsupported replacement path refuses. This specifies deduplication, not repeat-trial/regrant entitlement. The proposed staged startup below separates durable coverage preparation from final issuance, so activation need not know its actual T in advance. Neither a transport key nor a different attempt can mint another benefit for the same authorized business origin.

The Subscriptions [payer/plan admission fence](features/06-trials.md#single-episode-arbitration) precedes preparation and any trial-acquisition capture. Billing deduplication by purchase origin alone does not enforce no-repeat across two different Orders. The local fence and retained history are required for free-period trials as well.

**Proposed staged credit startup — initial access dependency remains gated by IA-2026-10-18**

1. Billing prepares durable coverage bound to the authorized acquisition origin, accepted policy/receipt/duration, frozen benefit scope and intended episode, retaining one preparation and reserved grant identity. Orders-origin identity uses its order/version/line; standalone acquisition is refused. Preparation supplies the required coverage admission evidence, not a balance, service proof, actual activation time or issued grant. Its consumer/validity and settlement fences require joint ratification; a detached read is not a substitute.
2. Subscriptions completes normal guards and obtains the separately required bounded Pricing activation authority. In its local S-Q01 transaction it fences/records the prepared coverage identity with the actual applied T, episode/revision and durable finalization outbox work. It consumes the final permit and applicable occupancy right as already specified. There is no remote Billing call inside that transaction and no cross-gear database transaction. Preparation cannot extend an expired final permit or bypass S-Q02 pre-capture protection.
3. Billing consumes/reconciles authoritative applied evidence and finalizes that **same grant** once, deriving original expiry from actual T and frozen duration. Lost replies and duplicate/reordered delivery recover the same identity and revision. Historical/already-applied grant recovery preserves T and keeps credit-dependent access pending until final evidence exists. This ordering does not authorize a new initial active commit while required access is pending: IA-2026-10-18 gates that profile until the readiness/coverage cycle is resolved. T is never moved to finalization or observation.
4. Failed/unapplied local activation cannot claim a final grant or served service. Subscriptions serializes an abort/non-application tombstone against activation; Billing settles preparation only from that authoritative fence. Unknown outcomes remain recoverable. If activation applied before cancellation, finalize the historical association with current closed/expired eligibility rather than erase the applied T or reopen access. Late finalization never resets T/expiry; an expired grant remains unspendable and accepted financial history is retained.

This startup ordering is proposed technical design, not an available provider capability or ratified protocol. A profile unable to bind prepared coverage, serialize application versus settlement and recover ordered same-grant finalization remains disabled. Grant finalization is not access permission by itself; other lifecycle/authorization/settlement guards still apply.

**Proposed allocation and recovery transaction**

1. Authenticate the caller and authorize the seller/payer/beneficiary scope. Resolve the canonical full Rating result and immutable accepted policy, then obtain the required lifecycle/served-time evidence. Separate original grant applicability from the corrected fact's frozen tariff. Missing or ambiguous crossing-boundary evidence stays pending and cannot be allocated wholly by arrival time.
2. Serialize affected grant/allocation revisions within Billing and compare the expected prior accepted result and financial lineage. Concurrent eligible items cannot both consume the last available credit. Ordering among competing allocations and multiple benefits must follow the supported explicit Pricing policy/contract. Stacking is unavailable in this release; no priority value is silently selected.
3. Replay the same accepted result version and digest as a no-op with its stored outcome. Same identity/version with different content is a conflict. A stale version cannot overwrite the head or restore credit again; recover the authoritative head. A required predecessor/manifest gap triggers source recovery instead of applying an independent delta.
4. Derive a replacement allocation from the full result and prior accepted allocation under frozen grant restrictions and authoritative effective boundaries. Eligible delay-caused trial gross 5 with remaining bonus 2 yields bonus 2/platform 3/customer 0; actual post-paid-boundary residual follows accepted paid terms. Preserve chronological expiry, early-conversion grant retention and closed-episode rules. Eligible pre-boundary trial gross corrected from 90 to 110 against grant 100 yields bonus 100/platform 10/customer 0 under the same full-result lineage. Liability/correction cases outside that scope remain pending for policy resolution.
5. Atomically commit the accepted result/allocation revision, bounded grant totals, operation receipt and durable financial/outcome work in Billing. History retains the replaced versions; no negative grant or fictional top-up is allowed. An unresolved replacement records its pending reason without claiming a fully applied new allocation or erasing the prior applied version.
6. Execute/recover invoice work through the established full-result/replacement contract: replace eligible drafts; use the standard versioned correction path for posted invoices. Keep an invoice-version guard and stable financial-work identity bound to allocation/result lineage. A duplicate or crash after a remote effect recovers that same effect/acknowledgement; it does not post a second credit or rerun grant issuance. Local allocation acceptance is not proof that a required Ledger posting completed. No cross-gear database transaction or inline posted-invoice rewrite is assumed.
7. Publish ordered versioned outcomes from the committed record. Readers can recover a snapshot at an immutable barrier plus subsequent outcomes; cursors bind authorized scope and explicitly report a gap/retention loss or partial page. Never translate lost history or unavailable balance into empty success, zero balance, permission or customer debt. Exact retention budgets belong to S-U11.
8. Consumers reconcile outcome versions with the current episode/paid state before any lifecycle intent. Corrected exhaustion never automatically reopens a closed trial; retained-grant exhaustion/expiry after paid conversion ends coverage only. Duplicate/reordered outcomes cannot duplicate or reverse conversion. Access enforcement and payment authorization remain separate contracts.

**Proposed typed outcomes**

| Outcome category | Required caller behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Prepared / finalization pending | Retain the same business/preparation/grant identity and phase; preparation is neither issued nor spendable. Recover authoritative application/finalization before credit-dependent materialization, without changing T or expiry |
| Applied / replayed | Retain the operation, grant/allocation revision and digest; financial completion may still be pending. A read of remaining balance is not an authorization token |
| Evidence unavailable / unresolved boundary | Recover authoritative Pricing/Rating/Subscriptions evidence; no default zero, guessed date, waived guard or fabricated customer obligation |
| Policy not supported / decision required | Refuse deferred trial exceptions even with explicit policy. Preserve authorization/financial guards; correction or liability outside the accepted rule keeps the affected replacement pending with prior applied lineage |
| Identity or payload conflict / scope-currency mismatch | Refuse mutation and expose the conflicting identity/version for authorized recovery; never create a substitute grant, perform FX or broaden applicability |
| Revision conflict / stale or gapped source | Read the current authoritative head or recover the missing source proof, then retry the same business operation; no blind overwrite or independent delta |
| Financial work pending / outcome unknown | Recover the exact invoice/Ledger operation and its durable acknowledgement; do not report posting complete, release protected rights, double collect or duplicate a correction |

This proposal aligns with [commercial-fact replacements and completeness](features/08-commercial-facts.md) and [consumer recovery](features/09-consumer-contracts.md). The mirrored Billing/Rating and Ledger contracts remain ratification work; historical Rating `Adjustment` delivery does not reintroduce a delta-only path here. No existing cash-funded Ledger API is designated as the promotional provider. A conformance fixture can exercise the proposed semantics before runtime work, but only provider/consumer implementation and joint evidence enable it.

## 5. Consumer lifecycle and validity

1. Read the existing Pricing PlanRevision with its optional trial block and obtain exact customer acceptance. The storefront uses Products descriptions plus Pricing version; customer dates and grants are separate records. Missing commercial inputs cannot become a free or credit offer.
2. For the credit variant, use the proposed §4 staged protocol to prepare durable coverage under the explicit authorized Orders business origin, accepted policy/duration and intended episode. Retain the same preparation/reserved grant identity; credit is not yet issued or spendable and actual T/expiry is not invented. Startup/cancellation fences require owner ratification; a provider without this capability gates the profile.
3. Where money is captured before a future start, secure the S-Q02 durable place and original future-term commitment **before capture**. This conditional path changes no supported unreserved admission and requires no capture for a bonus-funded trial, explicitly free trial or authorized postpay merely because the prepay profile captures money.
4. Complete normal authorization, resource/readiness and admission guards plus the variant's required evidence, then obtain final bounded activation authority. Within its original bounds, Subscriptions atomically consumes authority and the applicable reservation, or performs supported unreserved admission, while committing its actual S-Q01 boundary and, for credit, the prepared coverage identity plus finalization outbox work. Billing then finalizes the same grant from authoritative T and derives original expiry. Publish only actual applied commercial/served evidence. For initial activation, required usable access must precede local T: the described post-T finalization ordering cannot enable a credit-dependent initial profile until its joint readiness/coverage contract resolves that cycle. Already-applied recovery still retains pending grant work without moving historical T. No expired V1 hold/final permit is extended.
5. Free-period facts use accepted zero bindings. Credit-trial facts preserve ordinary tariff bindings and served history; Rating values them, and Billing allocates the shared bonus with invoice coordination. They do not imply the successor paid period or automatic customer liability.
6. For an active unconverted trial at duration expiry or accepted credit exhaustion, apply the normal guarded expiry/revocation or separately eligible paid transition. After early conversion has already applied, retained-grant expiry/exhaustion ends only bonus coverage; it does not itself stop or revoke paid service, duplicate conversion or reverse it. Remaining paid expenses follow the accepted paid-plan/settlement rules and applicable guards; no unsupported liability or waived payment prerequisite is inferred. Prepay needs prescribed payment; authorized postpay needs accepted terms and credit-service permission, not immediate payment/card. A balance event or bonus is neither permission. An earlier valid guarded conversion preserves the same unused grant for eligible paid expenses under its original expiry/applicability, without a new grant or trial extension; bonus coverage satisfies no upfront-payment guard unless the agreed settlement contract allows it. Postpay due/dunning remains unchanged.
7. Definitive failed prepay defaults to recorded failure/retry and normal expiry with retention, not paid start or automatic free extension. Unknown payment/balance stays recoverable and cannot duplicate collection/bonus allocation or release protected paid claims. Trial extension is deferred.
8. Later successful payment supports only a valid applied paid transition without waiting-gap accrual/backdating. Closed episodes/subscriptions do not reopen. Conversion, expiry, cancellation and ordered grant outcomes require guarded replay; corrected exhaustion cannot automatically restart access, replenish a grant or create a setup occurrence.

## 8. Ratification and enablement

Pricing and Subscriptions ratify the PlanRevision block, acceptance/evidence, exact variant schema and validity. Product policy owners approve initial applicability/eligibility and repeat refusal; Contracts participates only for explicitly bound terms. Billing ownership is accepted; S-U15 must ratify grant identity/scope, ordered recovery, canonical full-result allocation/correction, required Ledger integration, implementation of the selected eligible pre-boundary trial coverage rule including positive corrections, access enforcement and liability contracts outside that scope before credit launch. Early guarded conversion preserves unused credit under original grant restrictions/expiration; exact coordination remains S-U15 work. The policy-required/refusal framework, the v1 ceiling and the no-rollover/regrant/reactivation rule are accepted (2026-10-08); liability cases outside the eligible trial scope remain open decisions. Correction alone cannot reopen an exhausted closed episode or reverse an applied paid conversion.

Owners then implement and demonstrate the agreed cases and applicable S-Q01–S-Q06/S-U01/S-U04/S-U07/S-U12/S-U13/S-U15 gates. Direction approval is not other-owner sign-off, a new numbered Pricing decision, merged code or runtime evidence. General promotions, paid-trial policy, recurring allowances and prepaid wallets are not authorized by this focused extension.

**Credit preparation, application and delayed work**

The staged preparation in section 4 must provide commit-valid coverage for the exact episode/consumer/association. It cannot be withdrawn between local validation and application without an acknowledged consumer fence. Apply the FA-style bounded authority or equivalent consumption protocol: prepare outside the transaction; serialize local application against withdrawal; store actual T, original expiry policy, coverage reference and finalization outbox atomically; recover the winning receipt before abort/reassignment. A preparation timeout is not proof of non-application. Billing owns grant finalization and monetary history; no remote call occurs inside Subscriptions' transaction.

If application won, finalize the original grant from that applied evidence even when the acknowledgement arrives after cancellation or original expiry, preserving current closed eligibility and history. If a proven unapplied fence won, release only that preparation and trial-admission reservation. Neither a new transport key nor a replacement draft creates a new bonus. Credit-dependent access remains pending until final-grant evidence exists; a later cancellation/revocation wins over stale enable work. Do not move T or promise an extra trial interval because finalization was delayed.

Original grant expiry limits spend/service eligibility; it does not erase historical evidence. A late fact for an originally eligible served interval must be classified against the original interval and source, not its arrival date or today's zero spendable balance. Billing resolves historical allocation/correction on the same grant without making expired credit spendable again. Facts spanning trial/paid or grant-expiry boundaries require supported authoritative attribution; otherwise that allocation remains pending. No guessed prorating or shifted paid boundary is permitted.

Corrections retain canonical full-result and prior-allocation lineage. For supported eligible trial expenses before the applied paid boundary, bonus coverage is bounded and residual is platform-borne, including positive corrections and technical-delay overrun. Actual post-paid-boundary expenses use accepted paid terms and unchanged retained-grant restrictions. Other liability cases remain explicit policy gates; no general customer waiver is invented. Draft replacement and posted financial correction remain distinct, and local benefit allocation does not prove invoice/Ledger completion.

**Paid transition and access**

Validate the already accepted paid target and its current execution authority; do not demand new customer assent merely for an idempotent retry or ordinary conversion under unchanged accepted terms. Changed/unaccepted terms require new assent through a supported route. AP permit validity and FA financial permission are separate from historical assent. The supported conversion requires both, current local authorization/cancellation guards and one episode/aggregate winner. A future paid target may outlive the initial V1 hold; enablement therefore requires the separate target acceptance/permit extension, never renewal or reuse of the expired initial hold.

Initial free/bonus-funded trial access does not imply acquisition-time collection or a linked card. Any separately prescribed upfront payment follows AP/FA, but future paid-target payment is not silently moved to trial start. On paid conversion prepay needs prescribed-payment satisfaction and postpay needs accepted credit-service authorization. The grant is neither. Unknown permission cannot extend the trial; late payment cannot reopen an expired episode or backdate service.

Early valid conversion retains the same eligible unused grant until its original expiry, without resetting amount/duration or broadening its scope. Later exhaustion/expiry of that retained grant changes coverage only, not paid service state; a subsequent financial delinquency follows FA and the accepted lifecycle policy. Correction never restarts a closed episode or reverses an applied paid transition. Conversion itself is not a new setup-fee trigger.
