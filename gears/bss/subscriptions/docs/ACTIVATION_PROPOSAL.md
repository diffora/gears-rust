# Proposal: Subscriptions Activation Authority and Pricing Admission

**Additional scope reduction — user decision, 2026-10-15.** Defer trial extension and repeat trials, standalone acquisition without Orders, scheduled composition changes and scheduled resume, linked predecessor/successor subscription replacement, and changes of an existing subscription's base plan. Reject these operations before effects, including through operator/direct wrappers; earlier procedures and decisions for them are future traceability only. New paid and trial subscriptions originate through Orders. Existing-subscription lifecycle operations, supported immediate quantity increases/component additions, future order starts, cancellation scheduling, renewal and normal trial timers remain. Technical retry/successor attempts for the same acquisition remain recovery, not linked replacement of an existing subscription.

Free-period and credit trials, normal expiry/conversion and early conversion to the already accepted paid target remain in scope with their Pricing/Billing/financial gates. Trial-to-paid conversion is not a general changePlan capability. Preserve trial history/eligibility evidence and refuse repeat requests for the same payer/plan, including via a new subscription; a transport retry is not a repeat trial. No extension or repeat becomes available from a Pricing policy alone. Independent cancellation and new purchase have separate resource/data lifecycles: there is no automatic resource adoption, data transfer, place handover or continuity promise. Cancellation retains its existing retention/disposition rules; it does not mean immediate deletion. Unsupported plan changes cannot silently become cancel+new.

**Decision status: S-Q01 DIRECTION ACCEPTED by the user on 2026-10-07. Versioned provider/consumer contract and runtime evidence: OPEN.** This companion document records the selected direction and proposes the technical contract needed to implement it. It is not a fully ratified protocol, an implemented API, or evidence of approval by the external owning teams. Existing first-activation capability gates remain in force. The subsequent user-approved prepayment direction adds a separate durable place and Pricing/Products future-term commitment before actual capture; its implementation contract remains OPEN.

**Approval scope — 2026-10-07:** the user agreed that Workflow treats requested dates as scheduling lower bounds; Subscriptions completes required checks and resource readiness, obtains a proposed bounded Pricing permit for the original accepted terms, then assigns the commercial effective instant in its atomic transition to active. Expiry refuses activation and retries do not extend the interval. entitlement provider usable access may follow commercial activation. The worked example did not authorize a fixed two-minute TTL, a three-second entitlement provider SLO, or a credit policy. This records the original S-Q01 scope; the later prepayment approval below adds protection before capture without changing commercial T or final-permit expiry semantics.

The selected direction lets **Subscriptions assign the commercial activation effective instant in its successful atomic transition to active**, after required resource readiness. Current Pricing V1 exact-time holds cannot implement that direction unchanged: it requires an additive, versioned Pricing authorization capability and an agreed consumption protocol. Grant/retirement ordering, revocation, clock and durability rules, duplicate handling remain proposed technical details requiring owner agreement.
## 1. Context and source evidence

Three existing statements cannot all govern the same activation unchanged.

| Source | What it currently says | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Orders Lifecycle upstream §2.1](../../orders-lifecycle/docs/UPSTREAM_REQS.md#21-subscriptions) | An explicit activation start wins over an internally computed instant; the earlier quoted service date must not backdate billing or entitlement. | Baseline mismatch; AP01 now amends the Lifecycle/Workflow documents to lower-bound semantics. |
| [Subscriptions PRD](./PRD.md), [DESIGN §3.3.2](./DESIGN.md#332-pricing-acceptance-and-accepted-time) and PR #5131 source | The user-selected S-Q01 direction adopts the Subscriptions activation transition as the commercial start and retains distinct booking and acceptance instants; the versioned cross-owner contract remains open. | A caller timestamp chosen before asynchronous fulfillment cannot automatically stand for the eventual transition. |
| Current [Pricing SDK](../../pricing/pricing-sdk/src/acceptance.rs) and [fulfillment implementation](../../pricing/pricing/src/infra/commercial_terms/fulfilment.rs) | First hold freezes one `activation_at` and the original bindings. Both hold and fresh fulfillment eligibility must agree with that time. | A later Subscriptions-selected time cannot be substituted into the V1 contract. |

The Pricing behavior is established by implementation, not only by documentation:

- `PricingAcceptanceV1::hold` checks for an authorized exact command replay before fresh capture and again inside its transaction. Exact replay returns the historical held result even after its admission window has expired.
- A first hold, or a hold under another command key, performs fresh checks. Its transaction calls `eligible` and `same_activation`. `same_activation` rejects an activation timestamp different from the first stored hold with `AcceptanceMismatch`.
- `SellabilityV1::check_fulfilment` also calls `eligible` and **the same `same_activation` function**. A fresh read cannot authorize a newly selected timestamp after a different first hold.
- Fresh eligibility checks the original acceptance, tenant axes, terms digest, market, live SKU retirement and the current rows of the original accepted prices. It does not traverse successors or replace accepted bindings. The current price's own temporary or explicit end constrains eligibility; an informational successor cutoff alone is not that end.
- Current SKU state is read from Products during detached capture, before the Pricing transaction. `prices_unchanged` fences Pricing's local price rows only; it does not prove that a SKU could not retire between that Products read and a later grant decision.
- Current fresh admission requires server time before `hold_until` and `start_at <= activation_at < hold_until`. Price ends are exclusive too. Equality with an upper boundary is ineligible.
- `FulfilmentEligibility.checked_at` and `valid_before` describe an observation. The SDK explicitly excludes use as an admission token. Another hold key cannot move the first activation timestamp or extend its deadline.
- Pricing's acquisition identity is seller/catalog tenant plus order/version/line, without an execution attempt; the stored first hold is unique by acceptance. The proposed contract must preserve that acquisition-level uniqueness while binding the permitted consumer attempt.
- The current acceptance deadline is acceptance-time server `now` plus the selected seller hold duration, not the requested service date plus that duration ([issuance implementation](../../pricing/pricing/src/infra/commercial_terms/check.rs)). A bounded authorization cannot rescue a future schedule beyond the original deadline.

Consequently, neither replaying a hold nor calling the fresh read immediately before a local commit closes the authority gap. There is no unchanged-API production workaround asserted by this proposal.

[Orders Lifecycle's Workflow reconciliation](../../orders-lifecycle/docs/UPSTREAM_REQS.md#26-orders-workflow) additionally requires committed `report-spawn-signal` before activation dispatch, with ambiguous replies recovered; the cited Workflow PRD still describes the first intent as that signal. The same reconciliation records Workflow D-196 withdrawing the Catalog dependency graph; resolving that separate topology requirement is outside this activation package. This proposal preserves the common two-wave barrier and per-line outcomes, and aligns the signal-call documentation through AP06; it does not restore the withdrawn graph requirement. The committed signal fences direct order cancellation, not every later Workflow hold or mediated cancellation.

## 2. Decision drivers and scope

The protocol must preserve accepted money and product evidence, prevent activation before the requested schedule, distinguish an accepted operation from an applied transition, survive response loss and process restart, and give cancellation and activation one authoritative local winner. It must not require a shared cross-gear database transaction or hold a local database lock during remote calls.

The selected S-Q01 direction covers **first activation of one unchanged order line and execution attempt**, with immutable acceptance and composition evidence. The subsequent 2026-10-07 prepayment approval adds a confirmation stage before actual capture for confirmed/prepaid orders, potentially before a Subscription draft exists. That stage protects the place and future original terms; the later activation still determines commercial T. Standalone acquisition and import are deferred; the current acquisition profile requires Orders and its financial protection. It does not settle:

- Draft composition amendments that combine acquisition and delta acceptances, or replacement under a new business attempt. These remain independent S-U01 gates after direction approval and later S-Q01 contract ratification.
- Detailed S-Q02/S-U01/S-U07/S-U10 reservation, capture and release contracts. Core occupancy, suspended-place retention and the subsequent prepayment direction are accepted: active, retained suspended and order-reserved counts share capacity; confirmation secures an order claim before capture and activation consumes it once. Plain unconfirmed draft creation alone gives no automatic claim. The residual package (housekeeping draft cleanup, payment-deadline release through the order cancel route, future bookings counted from confirmation) was accepted on 2026-10-08; exact policy/key movement mappings remain open; no unconditional service is approved. Linked subscription replacement was subsequently deferred on 2026-10-15; technical attempt recovery remains distinct.
- One-time routing/accounting and occurrence-schema contracts, trial admission, retention charging or production budgets. S-Q03 valuation and S-Q04 durable identity per separately agreed chargeable action were accepted on 2026-10-07; S-U08/S-U13 contracts/runtime remain open. S-Q05 post-stop and confirmed cancellation charging-cutoff directions are separately accepted: no ordinary customer tariff for platform delay after the confirmed cutoff. Authorization/eligibility/durable-cutoff confirmation and pre-cutoff versioned withdrawal/rescheduling are now accepted; internal stops are split by cause and S-U05/S-U13 financial/stop/correction contracts remain open; S-Q06 optional PlanRevision free-period/credit variants and shared accounting [direction](TRIAL_PRICING_PROPOSAL.md) are accepted on 2026-10-08 alongside settlement/prepay defaults; Billing trial-bonus accounting/correction ownership is accepted, while post-expiry correction preserves original expiration and zero spend permission without trial extension, and correction cannot automatically reopen an exhausted closed episode or reverse paid conversion; early guarded conversion retains eligible unused credit on the same grant under original expiry/restrictions; eligible accepted-trial value, including positive corrections and delay/error overrun, served before the applied paid boundary is platform-borne, while actual post-boundary residual follows accepted paid terms; S-U15 provider/Ledger integration, boundary evidence, other liability/disposition and financial coverage/enforcement remain open; explicit Pricing-policy/refusal governs trial exceptions; the v1 ceiling is accepted; exact schema, retry/later paid routing, detailed postpay due/dunning and owner/runtime contracts remain open. Versioned renewal/retention configuration is accepted with frozen deadlines and v1 platform defaults; the PRD §7 profile is ratified as target v1 (2026-10-08) and unmeasured. These approvals do not enable fee-bearing capture/activation or waive pre-capture protection.
- A promise that all order lines begin simultaneously, or that commercial charging waits for observed usable access. Those would require additional product and integration decisions.

## 3. Alternatives

| Alternative | Advantages | Costs and failure boundary | Decision status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bounded activation authorization; Subscriptions assigns T during application** | Local state, effective start, consumption fence and downstream evidence have one atomic authority. Delays before application do not force a historical caller timestamp. Original money stays frozen. | Requires a new versioned Pricing capability, bounded reservation semantics, owner-approved treatment of catalog closure/revocation, clock rules and recovery retention. | **SELECTED DIRECTION — user approval 2026-10-07.** Versioned contract and implementation gates remain open. |

To treat that earlier timestamp as commercial start, Product/Orders/Subscriptions must explicitly accept the interval before local application; it does not satisfy the selected transition-owned start rule. | **Subscriptions chooses a point in time, then obtains a V1 hold, then applies** | Moves timestamp selection to Subscriptions without changing the hold shape. | The timestamp is selected before the remote hold and final local transaction. The selected direction changes admission semantics. If Pricing/Products require every ordinary catalog closure to revoke all previously issued authority immediately, the bounded local-consumption model below is insufficient. That requirement needs an acknowledged consumption fence or another agreed atomic authority; a fresh read or asynchronous event does not supply one.

## 4. Selected commercial time semantics

| Instant | Authority and intended meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Requested activation date/time | Caller intent and a lower bound for scheduling. It does not assert that service activated. Workflow retains its all-lines date barrier. |
| `contractEffectiveAt` | Contract booking/effectiveness evidence. It is not overwritten by fulfillment delay. |
| `resourceReadyAt` | Resource owner's effective readiness observation, with durable work-order/resource identity and version. Technical readiness does not itself prove granted or usable access. |
| **T — commercial activation effective instant** | Server-assigned by Subscriptions in the final guarded decision of the successful serialized activation transaction. It becomes authoritative only if that transaction commits. |
| `serviceActivatedAt` | Existing subscription field whose selected commercial meaning is T; the versioned integration contract remains open. It must not be presented as proof of access already observed at the point of use. |
| `usableAccessObservedAt` | Separately observed evidence of usable access, if the enforcement/resource owners provide it. This is a proposed observation concept, not a current SDK field or prerequisite endpoint. |
| `customerAcceptedAt` | Independent customer acceptance evidence under the applicable Contract/Orders flow. |

T is a **business effective instant recorded atomically with application**, not a claim to know the physical WAL/fsync instant. The still-proposed timing contract tests the authorization interval against the final local decision time T; that validity boundary requires owner agreement as described below. State, permit-consumption record, applied receipt, immutable revision, activation-driven facts/posture and outbox become durable together. A transaction that aborts has no activation at its sampled T.

A restarted or retried transaction must sample a new T after reacquiring its fences. It cannot reuse an earlier pre-expiry timestamp to enter an expired interval. When the original transaction already committed, recovery returns that original T instead of executing a new decision.

The distinction between decision time and durability needs explicit owner approval. Under the proposed semantics a valid final decision can become durably visible after its sampled T, including across the nominal upper boundary if durable completion was delayed. Owners must accept a bounded decision-to-durable-completion policy and its failure evidence. If instead authorization must remain valid at durable completion, this proposal is not closed: Pricing and the storage/platform owners must supply an enforceable alternative fence. A timestamp sampled before commit is not proof that persistence completed before expiry.

Clock uncertainty is also a release gate. Pricing defines the authorization interval in its authoritative clock domain; Subscriptions needs an agreed clock-error bound and conservative admission margins so its selected T is safely inside that interval. The final decision checks both scheduling/readiness lower bounds and the exclusive upper bound. Unknown clock health or exhausted safety margin refuses application. No fixed TTL, clock tolerance or commit-duration budget is invented here.

Entitlement provider remains asynchronous under the current target design. An activation commit records desired commercial posture and delivery intent, not proof that usable access already exists. Activation-driven billing evidence uses T according to accepted terms and the other billing gates. The user accepted that commercial T may precede usable access; this makes **no guarantee of “no charge until usable access”**. Remediation, credits and any entitlement provider latency commitment remain separate product/owner decisions. A stronger readiness guarantee requires a separate readiness/activation contract with the enforcement owners. No preparatory entitlement provider endpoint is assumed to exist.

## 5. Proposed bounded activation authorization

“Activation authorization” and “permit” below name **proposed business capabilities**, not current SDK types or method names. Section 13 specifies the additive/versioned semantic contract; owner SDK mapping and ratification are still required before enablement. Existing V1 receipt/hold semantics remain unchanged.

**Confirmed/prepaid future-order profile — additional accepted direction, technical contract OPEN.** Before actual Billing/Payments capture or settlement, order confirmation must secure both a distinct durable order/version/line capacity claim and a **durable Pricing/Products commitment** to the original commercial terms for the accepted future date and fulfillment window. This may precede Workflow wave-1 draft creation. The existing authorization-before-begin-fulfillment flow is not actual capture or evidence of this capability; seller tolerate-failure cannot waive the pre-capture guarantee. A preview, date check, acceptance receipt or V1 hold is insufficient. If original terms do not cover the agreed date/window, or either provider cannot guarantee the promise, capture must refuse or defer.

The commitment must retain original terms, covered future profile, acquisition identity, provider decision/version evidence and recoverable outcome. Ordinary catalog drift after it is granted must not silently invalidate or reprice a paid promise before the later permit. Pricing/Products must agree commitment issuance against retirement/closure, later permit derivation, revocation and cancellation. These are new shared capabilities under S-U01/S-U07/S-U10, not delivered V1 semantics. No shared cross-gear database transaction is assumed; owners must demonstrate durable capture/release fences rather than rely on earlier reads.

The order claim counts while held awaiting capture, remains protected while waiting for the future date and is consumed atomically into active occupancy without a second place. Paid rights are not released by ordinary reservation/hold TTL, unpaid-draft hygiene, ephemeral draft rebuild or unknown payment timeout. A paid unfulfilled order remains a remediation obligation or proceeds through explicit agreed cancellation with recorded financial remedy and fencing against late capture/activation. Billing/Payments owns the money; no refund amount/timing is selected. Unconfirmed unpaid draft cleanup is housekeeping; a confirmed unpaid order holds its claim until the versioned payment deadline and then releases only through the order cancel route. Attempt/version/replacement-draft association remains owner work. Neither confirmation nor prepayment asserts active service or emits activation-driven service facts.

**Final bounded permit.** After required readiness, Subscriptions requests the separate bounded authorization for the **original accepted bindings**. For the uncommitted ordinary admission profile, Pricing evaluates current eligibility and the original acceptance constraints. For an already committed paid future order, it derives eligibility from the protected commitment and its accepted constraints; it must not re-run ordinary live-catalog admission in a way that defeats that promise. Current security, cancellation, supported term/resource guards and the final interval still apply. A narrowly scoped permit is consumed once by the designated Subscriptions authority; the commitment itself is not that consumption permit.

The proposed issuance must order its authority against **Products SKU retirement as well as Pricing-local changes**. Pricing/Products must agree an authoritative reservation or version fence establishing whether the grant or retirement wins, with retained evidence bound to the issued right. The joint contract must define the separate commitment and final-permit issuance decision points. The ordinary fresh-issuance refusal rules below apply to an uncommitted acquisition, not to catalog drift after a paid future commitment. For the paid profile, the earlier authoritative commitment protects later permit issuance within the agreed profile; emergency revocation and actual expiry still require the agreed remediation/cancellation protocol. A detached V1 Products observation is insufficient; this is an additional owner-accepted capability gate, not a claim that a current port provides that fence. No shared cross-gear database transaction is assumed.

| Required evidence | Proposed invariant |
| --- | --- |
| Acceptance and commercial identity | Acceptance ID and terms digest; catalog/seller/payer/resource scope; original order ID, order version, attempt ID and line ID; target subscription and activation operation. Acceptance's existing order-line identity does not itself contain the complete local execution-attempt fence. |
| Intended consumer | One authenticated Subscriptions authority and its durable consumption namespace/fence. A copied receipt cannot authorize another service, subscription or attempt. All replicas share the same durable fence. |
| Frozen terms | Complete original binding identities/digests and immutable accepted descriptors, BillingTerms and policy evidence. A successor price or new catalog revision never reprices the authorization. |
| Allowed interval | A half-open `[L, E)` for the final permit. In the uncommitted ordinary profile it is bounded by original accepted `start_at`, `hold_until`, original-price starts/own ends and any narrower approved policy. In the paid future profile it is derived from the separate durable commitment's agreed future coverage and accepted constraints. That new contract does not retroactively extend an expired V1 hold or authorize beyond any applicable accepted bound. Scheduling/readiness lower bounds also apply locally. |
| Issuance and policy evidence | Durable final authorization identity, source/policy versions and original constraints. The paid profile additionally retains the pre-capture capacity claim, commercial commitment and payment outcome lineage; no capture is authorized from an uncommitted future quote. |
| Consumption evidence | Local successful activation identity, T, subscription revision, permit identity/digest and retained receipt/outbox lineage. Uniqueness covers the business activation fence as well as the permit ID. |
| Historical recovery | Authorized retrieval of the same original authorization/result and its accepted evidence after response loss, expiry or restart, for the agreed recovery and financial-evidence retention period. Retention is independent of admission expiry. |

The final upper bound uses the earliest applicable constraint of its agreed admission profile: original deadlines/price ends for ordinary uncommitted admission, or the separately accepted durable commitment coverage and its preserved constraints for a committed future order. It is not extended by command replay, a new transport key, provider restart or delayed resource readiness. Informational successor cutoffs are not substituted for actual original-binding ends. A request whose safe interval is empty is refused before local activation. Expiry does not authorize automatic re-acceptance or successor-price selection. For an already paid order, refusal to apply after true expiry preserves the commercial/payment obligation for remediation or agreed cancellation with recorded financial remedy; it does not release a place silently or justify fabricated activation.

The provider must fence issuance at the **acceptance/acquisition level**, in addition to command-key and exact consumer-attempt replay. The minimal proposed profile has one designated consumer fence/right for that acceptance; another command key or attempt cannot mint a concurrently usable right or reset already consumed acquisition authority. Same-right recovery retains the original interval. A different payload, designated consumer, attempt or target conflicts or enters an explicitly accepted predecessor/successor protocol; it cannot be treated as a harmless retry. The accepted successor-attempt semantics in §7 require predecessor settlement before any successor right; S-U01 still gates exact provider contracts and enablement. No conversion of an existing V1-held receipt into a window grant is implied.

**Ordinary changes after issuance.** The recommended reservation semantics honor a valid issued bounded authorization until its existing expiry despite an ordinary later price closure or SKU retirement. Such changes prevent new uncommitted authorizations, while previously reserved rights retain their original bounded terms. An already issued durable future commitment is a separate protected source for its later permit; ordinary drift cannot be treated as a new-acquisition refusal for that paid profile. If a later closure moves an original price's end earlier, owners must honor existing grants as reserved exceptions or make that closure effective for them only after expiry; they cannot both invalidate the right immediately and promise safe independent local consumption. This is a proposed change in the meaning of admission, requiring explicit Pricing and Products acceptance. It must not be inferred from today's hold or eligibility behavior. An already scheduled original-price end within the applicable accepted constraints still bounds E; providers cannot promise unsupported future coverage and discover the conflict only after capture. The durable future contract must establish valid coverage before capture, not rewrite an expired V1 acceptance later. Neither exception treatment nor deferred closure extends a final permit by replay or changes frozen money/terms.

**Emergency revocation.** An immediate remote revocation cannot safely coexist with independent local consumption through an asynchronous invalidation event alone. If required, Pricing/Products must deliver revocation through an acknowledged fence at the designated Subscriptions authority, or another accepted atomic authority. At that local fence, revocation and consumption serialize: revocation first prevents consumption; application first returns its durable applied outcome and requires a separate post-activation remedy. A provider-side “revoked” flag with no acknowledged consumer fence is not proof of immediate revocation. Emergency immediate revocation remains excluded/gated until this mechanism is specified and demonstrated; this proposal does not promise it.

Local order cancellation, withdrawal, stale-version and attempt settlement fences still apply even to an otherwise valid Pricing authorization. In the paid profile, cancellation/release must also serialize against late capture and reservation consumption through the owner-agreed protocol; a voided or absent draft alone does not release the distinct order right. The permit authorizes original commercial eligibility only. It cannot override current local state, Policy, occupancy, resource evidence or the authoritative order/attempt protocol.

## 6. Proposed sequence and local application

For initial trials, the [payer/plan trial-admission fence](features/06-trials.md#single-episode-arbitration) must be acquired before bonus preparation, resource work or trial-acquisition capture, and consumed with activation. This is additional initial eligibility, not a substitute for capacity/terms/payment guards; parallel Orders cannot each claim an independent trial.

All labels containing “proposed” in this sequence denote capabilities to be agreed. Remote work occurs outside the local database transaction.

1. For the confirmed/prepaid order profile, confirmation first establishes durable capacity and future terms before actual capture under the agreed payment/release fences. Missing either guarantee prevents capture, including with tolerate-failure. This reservation may predate a draft; an unknown payment outcome remains recoverable without releasing it. Thereafter Workflow preserves two-wave fulfillment: create every draft first, wait for the latest requested line date and its internal wave ordering, then submit activation intents. Before dispatch it must obtain/recover the committed `report-spawn-signal` required by the current Lifecycle owner contract; the reciprocal AP06 amendment now makes that dispatch prerequisite explicit. Under the selected direction the requested activation date is a scheduling lower bound, not the final commercial T. The signal closes only the documented direct-cancel boundary; Workflow hold/mediated cancellation still requires the agreed local fencing protocol.
2. Subscriptions authenticates before replay and recovers existing applied/settled outcomes before new effects. The activation operation carries exact order/version/attempt/line, subscription and expected local revision; accepted operation progress remains distinct from applied success.
3. **Before any resource provider resource effect**, Subscriptions obtains action-scoped, fail-closed Policy authorization and validates AMS tenant/delegation and current order/version/attempt evidence through the agreed owner contracts, outside a local database transaction. Denial, unavailable authorization or invalid required evidence prevents resource provider command dispatch and leaves an auditable blocked/refused operation. These checks are not an invented current reusable Policy token. After they permit the specific resource action, required readiness is established through the agreed resource provider path. This may already have external effects. Provisioning/readiness does not itself authorize paid service or activation facts. If a product cannot provision without exposing usable service before T, this staging profile is gated on an accepted resource provider/entitlement provider capability; no current prepare endpoint is invented. If later admission fails, prepared resource effects remain journaled for withdrawal, cleanup or remediation; no fictitious activation is committed to account for them.
4. Subscriptions obtains the proposed final authorization after readiness. An uncommitted path evaluates fresh eligibility; a paid future path supplies its earlier protected commitment so ordinary intervening catalog drift cannot negate the promise. A lost provider response is resolved by replay/read of that same business authorization; it never justifies a fresh extended window. Existing V1 holds do not supply this authority; no legacy hold conversion is planned.
5. Inside the local serialized transaction, Subscriptions checks the current operation, original acceptance/composition, order/attempt fence, withdrawal/cancellation state, authorization identity/consumer, fresh local security/Policy evidence, resource evidence and occupancy. For an order-backed reservation, application converts that exact claim into active occupancy, counting it once; supported unreserved profiles retain atomic admission. Upstream order-version/cancellation ordering must be bound to the local consumption fence by the agreed S-U01 protocol; an earlier remote order read alone cannot guarantee that fence.
6. At the final guarded decision, Subscriptions samples a new T and checks that it satisfies the requested barrier, required readiness, `[L, E)` and approved clock margins. It commits consumption, the transition to active, T and all associated local records atomically. No remote Pricing check or provider consume call occurs inside the database lock.
7. If the transaction aborts, no permit consumption or commercial activation exists locally; an associated order reservation remains held. A retry repeats the final guards with a new T. If the outcome is unknown, recover or settle the original transaction under the same local business fence before trying again; a plain not-found read does not prove rollback. An expired unused authorization cannot be consumed by recalling the earlier sampled time.
8. Any provider consumption acknowledgement needed for audit is delivered after the local commit with the same durable identity and recovered idempotently. Its exact contract remains part of the proposed capability; a lost acknowledgement cannot revoke an already applied activation or authorize a second one. The designated local fence remains the authority for whether this activation applied.

## 7. Boundary failures and recovery

Linked subscription replacement is deferred. This protocol applies to Orders-origin acquisitions and recovery of their attempts; it does not hand an existing subscription's resources, data or occupancy to a new sale. A successor execution attempt is not a successor active subscription.


| Boundary / race | Required result under the proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Requested date or required readiness has not been reached | Keep the operation pending and retain the paid order claim and original-term commitment. Date waiting or ordinary TTL is not release authority. Do not consume final authorization or publish activation facts. |
| First V1 hold fixed A; a call asks for B ≠ A | Current `hold` and `check_fulfilment` both reject the mismatch. Do not retry a different key as a workaround; the proposed capability is separate. |
| Exact V1 hold replay after expiry | Recover its historical result only. It does not prove fresh admission or provide a proposed authorization. |
| Uncommitted ordinary issuance refuses expired acceptance, original-price closure or retired SKU | No commercial activation or successor substitution. Do not capture for an unsupported future date/window; existing resource effects remain for remediation. An already committed paid profile follows its protected terms and is not rejected merely for later ordinary catalog drift. |
| Capacity/commercial commitment or final authorization response lost; capture outcome unknown | Recover the same business identity and evidence; an unknown capture does not mean unpaid and cannot release the claim. If the final interval expires, refuse application while retaining the paid obligation for remediation/agreed cancellation; never refresh by replay. |
| Local decision reaches E exactly, or clock uncertainty leaves no safe margin | Refuse; the upper boundary is exclusive. A request sent before E has no special right to make a new final decision at or after E; delayed durability follows the separate rule below. |
| Confirmed rollback, or process failure with an uncertain transaction outcome | Confirmed rollback creates no activation. An uncertain outcome is recovered/settled under the same local business fence before a fresh decision; a missing receipt read alone is not proof that a late commit cannot appear. Any permitted retry samples a new T; no stale pre-expiry timestamp is reused. |
| Local activation commits; response or event is lost | Recover the original applied receipt/T and replay the same outbox records. No second activation or reassigned start occurs. |
| Local final decision precedes E; durability is delayed past E | Apply only under the explicitly accepted decision-time validity policy and its bounded completion rules. If owners require durability-time validity, this scenario blocks protocol acceptance until an enforceable alternative exists. |
| Withdrawal/cancellation or settled-no-create wins the local activation fence | Reject subsequent activation consumption. A no-draft/void outcome alone does not release an order claim or settle unknown capture. Paid release requires agreed cancellation and recorded financial remedy, fenced against late capture/activation; late callbacks cannot resurrect a cancelled right. |
| Activation wins before withdrawal | Return the applied T and subscription. Withdrawal cannot rewrite it as never activated; an explicit cancellation/compensation operation is required. |
| Order version, attempt or frozen composition differs | Reject before consumption. An old authorization cannot be moved to a new order version, replacement draft or attempt. Concurrent upstream state changes need the agreed local fence, not a last-minute read alone. |
| Ordinary closure/retirement occurs after durable future commitment or after final permit issuance | Honor the paid commitment through its agreed future profile and derive its later permit accordingly; honor an issued final permit only inside its original interval. Prevent new uncommitted acquisition. Both behaviors require owner agreement; no current V1 workaround is asserted. |
| Emergency revocation has no acknowledged consumer fence | Do not claim immediate revocation support. That deployment/profile remains gated. A delayed invalidation event is not safe authority. |
| entitlement provider delivery/materialization is pending after activation | Preserve commercial T and explicit pending materialization. Apply accepted handoff monitoring/remediation; do not claim usable access or silently redate activation to eventual acknowledgement. |
| Same transport command or different key within the same business attempt | Recover/conflict under the same authorization and local activation fence; never extend the interval or create a second right. A new business attempt remains a separate S-U01 decision. |

**Proposed acquisition evidence and transition contract — S-U01/S-U07/S-U10**

This matrix makes the selected direction reviewable; semantic names below are not shipped endpoints or ratified wire types. Each command carries authenticated seller/payer/resource scope, stable business identity, expected relevant revisions, payload digest and correlation. Each receipt identifies the durable operation, exact accepted evidence versions, current disposition, outstanding owner effects and replay/conflict result. `pending` proves receipt of work, not protected capacity, capture, activation or compensation. An observation of another owner's state is not a fence against its concurrent mutation.

| Stage / proposed request | Evidence and owner boundary | Receipt, refusal and recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |

The proposed local projection distinguishes evidence pending, protected, capture pending/known, attempt attached, application pending/applied and settlement pending/settled. These are operation/evidence dispositions, not new subscription states or a distributed transaction. A version conflict returns the winning receipt and required recovery path; an unsupported predecessor policy returns explicit refusal. A new transport command cannot advance a business stage without its source evidence or reset original commitment/permit validity. Ordinary paid-right protection, suspended occupancy and trial prepared/final grant separation remain intact.

**Capture and cancellation arbitration — accepted semantics, 2026-10-14 (S-U01/S-U07/S-U10).** For an Orders-origin acquisition, Orders Lifecycle owns the durable decision authorizing capture to start and orders it against cancellation of that acquisition. Workflow delivers and recovers the work; Payments executes the money movement and supplies authoritative outcomes, while Billing owns the applicable financial remedy. Subscriptions owns the reservation and activation fence, and Pricing/Products own the durable original-term commitment. This is an agreed target protocol, not an existing Payments API: [Orders' current Payments request](../../orders-lifecycle/docs/UPSTREAM_REQS.md#25-payments) covers authorization and explicitly excludes capture/refunds.

1. Establish both durable protections before requesting capture start. Bind the capture business identity to the exact acquisition/order version, covered lines, financial obligation, protected claim and term-commitment versions, authenticated scope and payload digest. A whole-order capture requires protection for every covered line. These owner receipts must represent protections that cannot independently expire or be released while capture can still execute or its outcome is unknown; detached successful reads do not suffice. Unsupported revocation/release fencing blocks this profile.
2. Orders Lifecycle atomically records either cancellation winning before capture start, or capture-start authorization plus its durable dispatch intent, using the same acquisition/version concurrency guard. Validate the applicable order state and evidence in that local transaction; make no remote calls inside it. This is the logical start boundary, not proof that a bank has received a request or taken money. Authorization is bound to this capture identity and cannot be reused for another order version, line set or amount. A crash before commit creates no dispatch authority; a lost response after commit recovers the same decision and outbox work.
3. Payments accepts capture only under that recorded authority and deduplicates execution by the original capture business identity, independently of transport retries. Same identity with changed content conflicts. Its durable execution record and provider recovery must prevent a duplicate debit after a crash or lost response; a caller-side read or expiring HTTP idempotency cache alone is insufficient. Unknown remains pending recovery of this capture, never a new charge identity or definitive failure.
4. If cancellation wins before capture start, retain the cancellation decision so delayed start/dispatch commands cannot obtain or use authority and cannot debit. If capture start wins, cancellation remains available through the applicable order cancellation route; financial settlement can remain pending. Ask Payments to suppress outstanding execution where supported. Only authoritative, durable non-execution fencing permits a no-debit conclusion; otherwise recover the started capture, including a late success, and record the required Billing remedy. Starting capture adds no new prohibition on cancellation and gives no promise that money will not be taken after cancellation was requested. Existing direct versus workflow-mediated cancellation eligibility still applies.
5. Cancellation must also reach the Subscriptions operation/activation fence. A late activation cannot resurrect a cancelled acquisition; if activation already won its local fence, use normal applied-subscription cancellation/compensation. Release the unactivated acquisition's protection only through the coordinated release operation with authoritative capture resolution, acknowledged non-execution/no-additional-capture and activation/association fences, and the required recorded financial-remedy obligation. Unknown capture or activation retains protection. A draft void, payment deadline, cancellation notification or recorded remedy alone is not sufficient release evidence. A posted refund is not a universal prerequisite, and no refund amount or timing is selected here. Owner-specific release acknowledgements and financial evidence remain versioned contract work.

Standalone acquisition without Orders is deferred. Postpay without an acquisition-time debit continues under accepted terms and authoritative service authorization; it acquires no immediate capture or card prerequisite from this protocol.

**Capture race fixtures:** cancellation wins before start and delayed dispatch refuses; start wins then cancellation precedes provider dispatch; capture succeeds but its response is lost; restart after the Orders decision before dispatch; duplicate delivery and conflicting payload under one capture identity; unknown capture at the payment deadline; late capture success during cancellation; attempted claim/commitment release while capture is unresolved; cancellation racing activation; and multi-line capture with one missing protection. Assert one financial execution, retained protection while uncertain, durable winning receipts and separately visible cancellation/remedy completion. SDK encoding, mirrored owner acceptance, provider recovery and executed fixtures remain release gates.

**Execution retry and successor attempt — accepted semantics, 2026-10-14 (S-U01/S-U07/S-U10).** A technical retry keeps the original attempt and operation identities and recovers their durable outcomes. A new transport key does not create a new attempt, subscription, purchase, reservation, capture or charge occurrence. A new fulfillment attempt is a separately authorized successor operation, never an idempotent replay. The initial recovery profile preserves the same accepted order version/line and commercial acquisition; amendment, a new purchase after commercial cancellation and changed terms remain separately guarded operations.

1. Workflow recovers the predecessor by its exact business identity and all associated create, activation, external-effect and capture references. A missing notification/read, elapsed timeout or expired worker lease is not settlement. Resolve unknown application and financial outcomes before admitting the successor. If the predecessor already applied, recover that subscription and continue its existing fulfillment/remediation; do not create another subscription as a technical retry. A later applied cancellation does not retroactively turn it into an unapplied attempt.
2. For a genuinely unapplied predecessor, obtain durable create settlement and activation suppression serialized with the predecessor's local business fence. `settle_create` may prove `NoDraft` with a retained tombstone or find an existing draft; finding a draft is not itself proof of non-activation. Settle/void that draft and outstanding activation authority through the normal guarded path while preserving the separate acquisition claim and payment obligation. Retain the terminal non-application receipt and association generation so delayed create/activate commands cannot escape settlement. Settle or quarantine outstanding external work; resource reuse additionally requires effect-owner generation fencing so old cleanup cannot damage the successor.
3. Orders/Workflow authorize the successor for the same still-valid acquisition and expected predecessor/association revisions. Cancellation, a superseding order version or incompatible in-flight work refuses admission. Record predecessor-to-successor lineage and explicitly retain the original claim, original-term commitment and financial-obligation/capture references. Billing/Payments must confirm the existing financial disposition is applicable, including any pending cancellation/refund/credit remedy; a settled or remedy-bound payment cannot silently become available funding. Creating a successor is not permission to collect again or to invent a new setup occurrence.
4. Subscriptions atomically consumes the predecessor-settlement evidence and compare-and-swaps the acquisition association to the successor attempt, retaining the same claim identity and count, with the durable association receipt and outbox. Only one successor can win. Cancellation/withdrawal and reassociation must serialize at this same acquisition fence; upstream authority must be valid for that commit under the agreed protocol, not merely observed by a prior read. A failure before this local commit preserves the prior association and protected claim; a lost reply after commit recovers the same successor. This transaction changes local association and retained references, not remote money or Pricing records. Provider acknowledgements are coordinated outside it; partial/unknown provider association keeps successor activation pending without reopening the predecessor or releasing the claim.
5. Activation still needs readiness and the separately valid final Pricing permit bound to the designated successor. The predecessor authority must be unusable at its consumption fence before successor authority can be consumed. A new attempt cannot extend an expired permit, change original terms or create a concurrent usable right. If the original durable future commitment supports a later bounded permit, obtain it under the agreed provider contract; otherwise retain remediation/cancellation obligations. Rebinding never fabricates actual T or service during the failed attempt.

Standalone acquisition is deferred; this recovery protocol uses the original Orders acquisition identity. This decision closes the retry-versus-successor semantics; exact SDKs, provider mappings and mirrored owner acceptance remain S-U01/S-U07/S-U10 release gates. It neither enables amended-order association automatically nor allows terminal subscriptions or cancelled acquisitions to reopen.

**Successor-attempt fixtures:** lost create reply recovers the existing draft; late create loses to a no-create tombstone; unknown activation resolves to applied and prevents replacement; known unapplied activation permits fenced succession; two concurrent successor requests yield one association; crash before/after local rebind preserves or replays the correct association without a second claim; unknown capture blocks successor admission; late predecessor activation and cleanup cannot affect the successor; cancellation wins the admission fence; provider rebind response loss leaves successor pending; expired final permit is not renewed by retry; and an original payment under a remedy cannot be silently reused. Each case must expose the surviving acquisition, attempt, claim, payment and permit lineage. These are required acceptance scenarios, not executed runtime evidence.

**Commercial order-version amendment — accepted direction, 2026-10-14 (S-U01/S-U07/S-U10).** Changing accepted composition, price bindings or payer is a commercial amendment, not a technical retry or same-version successor attempt. [Orders' amendment contract](../../orders-lifecycle/docs/features/04-versioning.md#21-amend-a-committed-order) owns admissible states, authorized fields/actors, immutable version history and approval/acceptance handling. Its current PRD admits amendments in `submitted`, `pending_approval` and `approved`, not `in_fulfillment` or terminal states; this decision does not expand that window or authorize otherwise forbidden tenant-axis changes. Administrative corrections remain under Orders' existing non-commercial path.

1. Submit an explicit amendment against the expected current order version. Orders reruns the full commercial gate, captures the newly accepted bindings and evaluation evidence and obtains the required new-version approvals/acceptance; old approval or buyer acceptance is not silently inherited. A version commit alone is not proof of protected capacity, transferable payment or permission to activate. Preserve old versions, receipts and provenance rather than rewriting them with new content.
2. Before admitting capture or activation for the amended version, establish explicit predecessor/successor line mapping, scoped authorization and fresh applicability of occupancy policy, original-term commitment and financial evidence to that version. Retain, replace or move a claim only through the supported versioned mapping with exclusive ownership; an unchanged key must not double-count the same transferred right, while changed keys require destination admission. An old claim or Pricing commitment cannot be relabelled solely because the order ID is unchanged. New terms require the owner's new-version evidence, not silent repricing of the old paid promise. Unsupported mapping refuses/defers the affected protected-acquisition path before effects.
3. Billing/Payments resolves the old financial outcome and explicitly determines any applicable allocation, credit/refund obligation and separately authorized additional collection for the new version. No automatic reuse of the old capture, transfer of funds to a changed payer or debit of a computed order-total difference is authorized. Orders' indicative total is not a billing input. Preserve original payer/currency/capture lineage and recover unknown outcomes; a payment with an outstanding remedy cannot be silently counted as available funding. Each authorized financial effect retains its own business identity and correction lineage; a version increment alone earns no setup fee. Authorized postpay follows accepted new-version terms/credit authority without an invented immediate-payment requirement.
4. Supersession must fence old-version capture dispatch and activation at their effect owners as well as rejecting stale Orders callbacks. If old capture has started or its outcome is unknown, use the accepted capture/cancellation recovery protocol; a new version cannot erase the debt, paid promise or remedy obligation. A stale-version read or asynchronous event alone is not an execution fence. New-version capture/activation remains pending until the required owner evidence and association are established. This is coordinated durable work, not a transaction across Orders, Subscriptions, Pricing and Payments.
5. Failed preparation before Orders' version commit leaves the prior version and protected claims intact. After the new version commits, incomplete provider handover retains old protected obligations with explicit pending disposition, while the superseded version cannot resume fulfillment merely because its claim remains. Do not automatically roll back the order version or discard old protection on timeout. Release/replace the old claim only after acknowledged capture/activation fences, applicable financial resolution/remedy and the supported successor mapping or explicit cancellation conditions. No blanket requirement to wait for a posted refund is added. Exact provider sequencing, mappings and financial allocation policy remain owner contract work; unsupported cases stay gated.

This direction separates commercial reassessment from technical recovery. It does not enable amendments during fulfillment, reinterpret an existing active-subscription change as an order amendment, change the increases/additions-only initial quantity-change scope, or create a generic payer-funds transfer facility.

**Order-version fixtures:** unchanged-content replay of an amendment returns its original version/result; conflicting payload under the same identity refuses; failed new-version preparation preserves the old claim; old approval/acceptance and late capture/activation cannot authorize the new version; changed occupancy key without capacity does not discard the protected source; lost provider handover response remains pending and recoverable; unknown old capture prevents assumed funding; changed payer cannot inherit the old payer's payment automatically; amendment racing capture observes the capture-start boundary; and `in_fulfillment` amendment refuses under Orders' existing state guard. Required owner/provider fixtures are not claims of runtime support.

**Required fixtures:** V01–V04/V06 cover lost confirmation/capture/create replies, capture racing release, restart after local commit, final-permit expiry after payment, refused new-attempt rebind, failed claim move, and two lines with independent starts/partial remediation. Include standalone-create refusal before effects and lost-reply replay for the authorized Orders origin. For each crash point, record the surviving owner references, expected receipt and which effect is still pending. Delivery, SDK encoding and owner acknowledgements must be ratified and exercised before this profile is enabled.

## 8. Two-line order and partial fulfillment

For a prepaid order, both line claims and their future-term commitments must already have been secured before capture; a line failure does not erase that paid obligation. Assume independent lines A and B have both reached `draft_created`, and Workflow's common date barrier has passed. Each line receives its own activation operation and proposed bounded authorization. They can apply at distinct instants **T_A** and **T_B**. Waiting until the latest requested date prevents early dispatch; it does not create a shared database commit or guarantee equal starts.

If A applies and B fails before application, Workflow's documented default is **remediate**: preserve A's applied outcome, halt affected pending work, allow independent in-flight work to settle, create the required manual task and hold the order. The order is not `completed` while B is unactivated. A is not automatically cancelled solely because B failed. This description requires no restored Catalog dependency graph.

If remediation succeeds within the already accepted protocol, B gets its actual successful T_B and the order can complete once every line is applied. Recovery cannot change A's T_A to T_B for cosmetic order-level alignment. If B's original authority has expired, this proposal does not grant an extension or resolve new-attempt/re-acceptance policy; the case remains pending owner/operator resolution under S-U01.

If fail-fast was configured, or remediation is exhausted and Workflow decides failure, it reconciles every uncertain line and compensates the real outcomes: void unapplied drafts while separately resolving their protected order claims and payment obligations, cancel applied subscriptions through Subscriptions, and account for late capture/activation successes. A paid unfulfilled line requires explicit agreed cancellation with recorded financial remedy before its reservation is released; this does not prescribe refund amount or timing. Commercial history and consumed usage remain intact. The financial correction policy remains with Rating/Billing and the existing one-time/retention gates. No ETF or unused-portion credit is derived merely from `order_compensation`; required reversal/correction evidence is a separate outcome.

Thus “all lines required for order completion” is preserved, while per-line service and financial evidence remain factual. A requirement for all-or-none simultaneous commercial service, or for free service during remediation, would be a new product/protocol decision rather than an interpretation of the existing two-wave saga.

**Direction approval is recorded above; detailed protocol ratification remains open.** The owning teams must still agree one versioned provider/consumer contract, its source changes and required conformance evidence. 1. **Product meaning — directions accepted by the user:** confirmation secures a durable place and future original terms before actual capture for the confirmed/prepaid order profile. 3. **Time validity:** owners must choose final decision time versus durable completion as the validity boundary, and agree the clock margins, transaction/restart behavior and observable evidence that make the choice enforceable.
4. **Revocation:** owners must agree ordinary reserved-right behavior and either specify an acknowledged fence for emergency immediate revocation with required conformance evidence, or explicitly exclude it from the intended profile. No event/read approximation is allowed.
5. **Local consumption:** owners must specify the durable local fences shared by cancellation, withdrawal, stale version/attempt, occupancy and application, including pre-capture place/terms evidence, actual payment outcome, own-reservation conversion and upstream order-state ordering. Capture/cancel/release races require an agreed durable protocol; a prior read or seller tolerate-failure setting is insufficient.
6. **Recovery and retention:** owners must specify exact provider/local replay, unknown payment and activation outcomes, paid rights surviving ordinary TTL/date waiting/draft rebuild, non-extension of final permits, evidence retention and consumption reporting. Unconfirmed unpaid draft cleanup is housekeeping with a configurable TTL and no financial effect; the payment-deadline release goes through the order cancel route (accepted 2026-10-08). Paid unfulfilled release needs agreed cancellation and recorded financial remedy; no automatic refund amount/timing is specified.
7. **Order outcomes:** owners must agree independent per-line T values, all-lines completion and default remediation with joint expected test outcomes.
8. **Conformance contract:** owners must agree the joint scenarios above, their expected outcomes and the evidence each implementation must produce. Implementation readiness is evaluated separately after the provider and consumer exist.

**Occupancy read and caller predicate**

The target batched read binds the exact payer/resource/scope key and policy version to separate active, retained-suspended and confirmed-reserved counts, an explicit limit, observation/recovery provenance and authorized evidence identifying which caller claims are already represented. Active count keeps its literal meaning. A plain draft_count is diagnostic only and cannot substitute for reserved count.

For a new unreserved purchase, assess existing protected occupancy plus this request's additional unrepresented rights and relevant Orders in-flight claims not already represented in the provider result. Count each business right once; unresolved identity/dedup evidence blocks new admission. Preserve Orders' own in-flight uniqueness rule. Never re-bucket a provider's key locally or infer limit one. An old active-only adapter is unsupported for this profile, not a partial successful answer.

A preflight pass is not reservation. Confirmation must acquire each required durable claim before capture. Later activation of its own valid reservation and unchanged-key resume are conversions of protected rights, not new competitions for capacity; a later reduced limit cannot revoke them. Workflow validates own-claim evidence instead of treating that reservation as a collision with itself.
