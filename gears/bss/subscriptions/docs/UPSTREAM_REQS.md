## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

This register names the requirements consumers place on Subscriptions and the provider work those requirements depend on. Sources and status meanings are frozen in [RECONCILIATION](RECONCILIATION.md). Every incoming requirement below is covered by the [PRD](PRD.md); implementation belongs to [DESIGN](DESIGN.md) and [features](DECOMPOSITION.md). TARGET means proposed documentation, not delivered code or another owner's approval.

## 2. Requirements

### 2.1 Orders operations

#### Durable operation protocol

- [ ] `p1` - **ID**: `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-upreq-order-intents`

Subscriptions MUST preserve separately callable draft creation and activation; retain order/version/line/attempt identity; return durable acceptance separately from terminal outcome; support replay, withdrawal and status recovery. Settlement of an uncertain create MUST prevent a delayed duplicate from escaping compensation. Creation deduplicates on `(order_id, order_version, attempt_id, line_id)`; transport retries within that attempt return the same operation. A new attempt is not a replay and requires the section 7 predecessor-settlement and exclusive successor-correlation protocol, preventing a second live acquisition; the profile remains unavailable until its versioned provider mapping is delivered. Draft composition changes additionally require the accepted S-U01 protocol for immutable original acquisition plus delta evidence, first hold and withdrawal; until then that source-state profile is unavailable. No quoted-date backdating is allowed; S-Q01 direction is accepted, while its shared clock and authorization contracts remain open. The subsequent prepayment direction requires order confirmation to secure both a durable order/version/line place and original future-term commitment before actual Billing/Payments capture, potentially before a draft exists. Existing payment authorization is not capture or proof of this capability, and seller tolerate-failure does not waive it. A draft settlement/void or rebuild cannot erase the separate claim or paid obligation. Capture, cancellation, release and activation need owner-agreed fencing, idempotency and unknown-outcome recovery under S-U01/S-U07/S-U10; attempt/version or replacement-draft association remains gated.

**Reciprocal documentation package, 2026-10-15:** [AP01-AP07 and AP-C01-AP-C12](ACTIVATION_PROPOSAL.md#13-orders-and-pricing-reciprocal-activation-contract) are specified within Subscriptions, with required Orders Lifecycle/Workflow and Pricing changes in the [component proposals](proposals/README.md). Their own documents remain unchanged; reciprocal adoption, exact versioned SDKs, Products/financial mappings, runtime conformance and independent owner ratification remain open. V1 is unchanged.

**Accepted capture arbitration, 2026-10-14 (S-U01/S-U07/S-U10):** Orders Lifecycle owns the Orders-origin local acquisition/version decision ordering cancellation against capture start and persists authorized dispatch atomically. Workflow delivers/reconciles; Payments executes once per capture business identity and recovers uncertain outcomes. Both claim and original-term protection must already exist and remain protected through uncertainty. Cancellation first prevents debit; start first preserves cancellation with pending financial recovery/remedy. Coordinated release needs authoritative capture resolution, acknowledged capture/activation fences and the applicable recorded remedy, without a universal posted-refund prerequisite. The [accepted protocol and race fixtures](ACTIVATION_PROPOSAL.md#7-boundary-failures-and-recovery) close this semantic ownership/ordering choice; mirrored owner contracts, direct-path designated-owner mapping, provider evidence and runtime acceptance remain OPEN. Current Orders authorization-only requirements do not supply this capability.

**Accepted successor-attempt semantics, 2026-10-14 (S-U01/S-U07/S-U10):** technical execution retry retains the original attempt. A separately authorized successor for the same accepted acquisition requires resolved predecessor application/financial outcomes, durable non-application and create/activation suppression, exclusive versioned predecessor/successor association and retained claim/commitment/payment lineage. The local association commit preserves claim identity/count and publishes a durable receipt/outbox; remote mappings remain staged and gate activation. Already applied predecessors are recovered, not recreated; existing payment remedies and final permit bounds remain binding. The [protocol and required fixtures](ACTIVATION_PROPOSAL.md#7-boundary-failures-and-recovery) select the semantics, while exact SDKs, provider mapping, amended-version policy and runtime acceptance remain OPEN. No new capture, setup occurrence or reopening of a cancelled acquisition follows merely from succession.

**Accepted commercial amendment boundary, 2026-10-14 (S-U01/S-U07/S-U10):** changed composition, price bindings or payer require Orders' commercial amendment process and new-version evidence, not a technical retry. Existing admissible states/fields and required approval/acceptance rules remain authoritative. Prior claims, commitments and payments are not automatically inherited; explicit line/claim mapping, destination capacity where applicable and Billing/Payments funding/remedy applicability are prerequisites for the amended protected acquisition. Fence superseded capture/activation, recover old uncertain outcomes and preserve old obligations through failed or partial handover. The [amendment protocol](ACTIVATION_PROPOSAL.md#7-boundary-failures-and-recovery) selects this semantic boundary; exact SDKs, mappings, financial allocation and provider acceptance remain OPEN. It does not enable `in_fulfillment` amendments or automatic cross-payer fund transfers.

- **Source**: [Orders requests §2.1](../../orders-lifecycle/docs/UPSTREAM_REQS.md#21-subscriptions), [Workflow PRD](../../orders-workflow/docs/PRD.md), PR5131 SUB-O1–O3/O10–O16; Atlas C03.
- **Status / owner**: TARGET, Subscriptions. S-Q01 activation direction is user-accepted; the versioned authorization and clock/fencing contracts are JOINT OPEN with Orders, Workflow and Pricing.
- **Acceptance**: V01/V02 exercise timeout settlement, terminal outcomes and compensation on both sides; every order line is accounted for before acknowledgement.

#### Occupancy and atomic entry

- [ ] `p1` - **ID**: `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-upreq-occupancy`

Subscriptions MUST expose the authoritative `(payerTenantId, resourceTenantId, overlapScopeKey)`, explicit `maxConcurrentActive` and versioned policy provenance. Active subscriptions, retained suspended places and explicit order reservations MUST be reported separately and all counted against admission capacity, including reservations awaiting capture. Each right counts once. `activeCount` remains the active-only count; paid drafts are not active. The target versioned read must provide all three counts and coherent count/key/policy provenance. Orders' existing active-only request and its proposal to release a slot when leaving active require explicit bilateral alignment; the owner request is not silently rewritten, and the combined-count read remains a proposed versioned contract rather than a shipped API. Missing key/limit evidence MUST refuse admission rather than default to one.

Suspend retains the place; resume converts the same retained place to active without a second place; applied cancel releases it. Pending, failed or scheduled cancellation or mere dwell/TTL expiry does not release a place while status remains suspended. The same policy MUST be enforced atomically with admission or an active/retained key change, counting the target once. Under the accepted 2026-10-18 rule, suspended seat purchases, quantity increases and component additions refuse; a separate authorized guarded resume must precede a normal active change. Only an admitted active composition change may move its claim to the resulting occupancy key, with atomic destination admission; failure preserves the old claim. Tenant, payer and ownership transfer remain deferred. An unchanged-key place is protected from competing acquisitions, but payment, Policy, term and resource guards still govern local resume. Entitlements reinstatement/materialization remains asynchronous and separately observed; retention adds no synchronous access-ready gate and does not assert usable access. `bill`/`waive` remains independent. A versioned limit decrease preserves all existing protected active, retained-suspended and explicit order-reserved claims, even when their total exceeds the new limit; it never evicts or releases a claim solely for that change. Reject new admissions that would exceed the current limit until room exists. Unchanged-key resume and activation consuming an existing reservation convert the same protected right without another slot; their other guards remain. Applied cancellation releases its own claim. Policy/key/claim movement requires an established destination/version mapping and atomic move; unsupported or failed movement preserves the source claim and refuses the move. Exact provider contracts remain open; import mapping applies only to the separate import capability. A plain unconfirmed draft alone creates no reservation. Confirmation MUST secure a distinct durable order/version/line claim before capture, potentially before draft creation; activation consumes that claim atomically without taking a second place. Paid rights survive ordinary TTL/date waiting, unpaid-draft hygiene and ephemeral draft rebuild; unknown payment cannot release them. Paid fulfillment failure requires remediation or explicit agreed cancellation with recorded financial remedy, fenced against late capture/activation. Unconfirmed unpaid draft cleanup is housekeeping with a configurable TTL and no financial effect; a confirmed unpaid prepay order holds its claim until the versioned payment deadline from the accepted order terms and then releases only through the order's cancel route (accepted 2026-10-08). The versioned lifecycle/payment protocol remains open.

- **Source**: Orders SUB-O5, D-179/D-180; Atlas D03/C03 uses a different key and draft rule.
- **Status / owner**: S-Q02 core, suspended-place and subsequent prepayment directions ACCEPTED by the user on 2026-10-07; versioned owner/payment/release contracts and runtime evidence JOINT OPEN under S-U01/S-U07/S-U10. Limit-decrease claim preservation is ACCEPTED on 2026-10-08; the residual package (housekeeping draft cleanup with configurable TTL, payment-deadline release through the order cancel route, future bookings counted from confirmation) is ACCEPTED on 2026-10-08; exact policy/key movement mappings and runtime remain open. Linked subscription replacement was subsequently deferred on 2026-10-15; technical attempt recovery remains distinct.
- **Acceptance**: V03 includes partner resource tenants, Orders-origin acquisitions, resume and supported composition key changes; standalone acquisition and tenant transfer are deferred. Active 0 plus retained suspended 1 under limit 1 refuses a competing acquisition; resume converts the retained place once, while pending/failed cancel preserves it and applied cancel releases it. Failed destination admission preserves the old claim. Consumers use all three counts and the enforced key/limit provenance without re-bucketing or defaulting missing policy. A held order reservation also blocks competing capture/admission and converts to active once. Plain draft creation alone does not establish a reserved place; a paid order does not lose its distinct claim on draft cleanup or unknown payment.

#### Atomic composition change

- [ ] `p1` - **ID**: `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-upreq-composite-change`

Subscriptions MUST apply an admitted multi-item change to one subscription atomically against its expected revision. **Accepted 2026-10-18:** new seat purchases, increases and additions to suspended subscriptions MUST refuse at submission and commit, even though suspended is non-terminal (SUB-D-39). Require a separate authorized guarded resume, then normal active change; no implicit resume or queued purchase. S-U01 draft changes remain separately gated. If suspension wins the commit race, recover staged external effects without applying the purchase. If the purchase committed first, preserve its accepted history/obligations and reconcile stale materialization under the newer suspension fence. This does not prohibit renewal recovery or unrelated administration, or add synchronous entitlement readiness. Resulting bounds, eligibility and overlap are validated together. The operation carries per-line provenance and one effective instant. Unsupported multi-date requests MUST refuse before partial work.

- **Source**: [Changes PRD](../../orders-changes/docs/PRD.md), CHG-S1–S5, PR5131 `fr-composite-change`.
- **Status / owner**: TARGET, Subscriptions; first supported commercial profile is constrained by Pricing, the accepted S-Q02 core/suspended-place/prepayment directions and their still-open owner/residual-rule contracts.
- **Acceptance**: V06 demonstrates suspended-purchase refusal at submission/commit, separate guarded resume before active change, both orderings of the suspension race, and stale revision, failed bounds and different dates leaving no partial commercial change. [OL-P08 owner handoff](proposals/orders-lifecycle-proposal.md#required-changes) requires Orders Changes to amend §5.1, §6.1 Target Eligibility and §9.2 CHG-S2: non-terminal alone is insufficient and cannot authorize a suspended purchase in this Subscriptions profile. External-owner acceptance remains OPEN; local refusal already applies without waiting for that acceptance. The existing draft capability gate remains.

### 2.2 Pricing and commercial identity

#### Accepted commercial inputs

- [ ] `p1` - **ID**: `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-upreq-accepted-bindings`

Subscriptions MUST preserve accepted price, product, policy, invoice and billing-term evidence. It MUST resolve renewal and `ends_on` boundaries into complete effective-dated slices and retain immutable replay inputs. A monetary version cannot define economic occurrence identity. Paid future orders additionally require durable Pricing/Products commitment to the original terms before capture, covering the accepted date/fulfillment window and protected from ordinary later catalog drift. The later bounded permit remains separate and non-extendable by replay. Preview, an eligibility observation or current V1 hold is insufficient; absent supported coverage, capture refuses/defers. Detailed commitment/revocation and paid-remedy protocols remain S-U01/S-U07 gates.

**Mid-term continuation accepted, 2026-10-16.** Automatic adoption at `ends_on` requires explicitly accepted continuation policy and satisfied notice/assent prerequisites (SUB-D-41). Pricing closure/selection alone is insufficient. Subscriptions owns complete successor facts and exact seals; unresolved continuation keeps the affected full valuation pending. The Pricing observation/committed-read guarantee, discrepancy/reconciliation SDK mapping and joint finalization evidence remain OPEN under S-U08/S-U13; call receipt time or TTL is not a freshness proof. See the [proposed Rating protocol](proposals/rating-proposal.md#binding-authority-and-calculation-evidence).

**Explicit unresolved successor accepted, 2026-10-16.** A known unresolved continuation of an established obligation MUST be represented by a full successor fact and exact successor seal (SUB-D-42). Preserve known terms, geometry and affected purchase/lot/quantity evidence; identify the unresolved interval with reason and boundary evidence. Commercial publication is valid while full Rating valuation remains pending. Resolution requires another fact/seal version. An old result cannot satisfy the newer seal, and no automatic posted reversal or waiver of admission prerequisites follows.

**One-time basis accepted, 2026-10-16.** Qualifying actions carry applied evidence and `Once` for flat or `Units { quantity, unit }` for per-unit, with positive exact frozen action quantity (SUB-D-43). Rating validates against accepted terms and refuses missing quantity/model/unit mismatch; no implicit one, seat multiplier for flat or current-subscription quantity lookup. Original-action correction preserves occurrence and advances fact/seal; distinct additional fees require distinct agreed actions. SDK mapping and RT-C16 execution remain S-U08/S-U13 work.

**Flat/floor arithmetic accepted, 2026-10-16.** S-U08/S-U13 adopts recurring flat once per component and covered time under identical applicable terms, not per seat/lot; different simultaneous flat tariffs remain unsupported (SUB-D-46). Recurring proration and supported period floors use exact covered UTC seconds over declared billing-period UTC seconds. Rating sums eligible base charges and applies one minimum per price/subscription/period/currency/scale over union coverage; different price IDs retain separate floors. Cross-fact aggregation remains open, with no accepted admission ban or independent per-fact substitution. Current Pricing rejects hourly/resource-scoped usage minimums; PC-P07 must address the required per-resource profile with separately agreed window/rules. One-time scope is unchanged. R-30/R-34 owner adoption, SDK mapping and RT-C04 execution remain delivery gates; the local math decision is accepted.

**Per-resource minimum required, 2026-10-16.** The Jelastic use case requires resource-specific minima in addition to the accepted pooled profile. Clarified on 2026-10-17: the scope selects eligible consumption (for example storage or traffic) and an individual container or a node group pooling container storage. The user selected individual/group scope as alternative tariff settings on 2026-10-17; the same eligible usage does not receive both minima. Cross-price eligibility and historical membership contracts remain open. The minimum is monetary, as selected on 2026-10-17, and applies after rating eligible usage in the selected scope. The tariff selects an hourly or billing-period minimum, accepted on 2026-10-17. Accepted 2026-10-18: a partial eligible hour retains the full hourly monetary minimum once per selected target/window, without time proration or multiplication by group members. The user also selected calendar-hour windows: 10:50–11:10 covers two hourly minima, each compared with its own eligible base; excess in one hour does not offset the other. Accepted 2026-10-18: partial billing-period minimum treatment is an explicit tariff setting, full minimum or proportional to eligible time, preserved in accepted terms. The full minimum for partial calendar hours is unchanged. Exact resource/group identities, timezone/SDK mapping and eligible-duration evidence remain open. Pricing PC-P07, Rating and Subscriptions must jointly define immutable policy/acceptance, calculation, resource inventory/ownership/coverage and replay/correction evidence. Current D-504 refusal is a provider gap, not rejection of the product requirement; invoice-line grouping does not select minimum scope. No existing pooled terms are silently reinterpreted (SUB-D-47, SUB-D-48, SUB-D-49, SUB-D-50, SUB-D-51, SUB-D-52, SUB-D-53, SUB-D-54).

**Empty versus zero-valued result accepted, 2026-10-16.** S-U08/S-U13 MUST distinguish a complete empty replacement removing only the fact's prior contributions under normal Billing guards, active zero-valued lines preserving classification/terms/provenance, and structured pending without complete monetary delivery (SUB-D-45). Proven zero usage requires completeness and applicable tariff/minimum-fee evaluation. A zero total cannot discard lines; empty/zero is not financial completion. Exact versioned flag/codec mapping and RT-C06/RB-C03 execution remain open.

**Rating pending reasons accepted, 2026-10-16.** S-U08/S-U13 SDK mapping MUST expose exact-fact status with a nonempty structured list of blockers for missing conditions/data: stable code, affected scope/dependency, responsible source, recoverable evidence and resolution route (SUB-D-44). Rating links source `Unresolved` reasons and durably stores its own findings. Previous complete results identify their source version and do not imply current completion. Exact operations/codecs and RT-C12 execution remain open.

- **Source**: Pricing D-501–D-510/D-520, Subscriptions SUB-D-29, Rating T-D-37 and proposed R-29.
- **Status / owner**: Pricing provider CURRENT; Subscriptions adapter TARGET; S-Q01/S-Q04 technical contracts and S-U08 gates affected migrations and separately priced quantity increases within one logical item.
- **Acceptance**: V04/V05/V08 compare accepted bindings before and after catalog changes and replay historical periods without current descriptor substitution.

### 2.5 Provider prerequisites and bilateral acceptance

These are requests to other owners, not assertions of their implementation. The incoming requirements above remain the traceable product obligations.

**Migration scope — user decision, 2026-10-14:** migration from a predecessor deployed subscription/billing model is not required and is outside this design's delivery scope. No historical database conversion, old/new billing cutover, legacy occurrence remapping or migration execution plan is a completion or launch prerequisite. Import/adoption is also deferred by the subsequent user instruction; neither capability is allocated in the current version. Ordinary supported tenant/claim movement, API versioning and the documentation identifier cross-reference register remain distinct concerns; their names do not imply a legacy-system migration project. Earlier migration gates are superseded to this extent.

**Import deferred — user decision, 2026-10-14:** import/adoption of existing subscriptions is not part of the current version and is not a design-completion or launch prerequisite. Its retained IDs, historical examples and V15 identify future work only; no import endpoint, adoption workflow, legacy identity store or import fixture is allocated for current implementation. Ordinary new acquisition, tenant ownership/transfer and current-system correction/recovery retain their existing scope. This supersedes the earlier same-day statement retaining import while excluding migration.

**Current delivery scope — user decision, 2026-10-15.** Defer the audit subsystem (to be built later from events), separate customer acceptance confirmation (`confirmAcceptance`), Contracts-authored multi-step ramps, co-termination/date alignment, collection pause/resume, all customer notification scheduling/delivery, payer/resource-owner transfer, administrative historical correction/backdating, and per-sale brand/PlanTier metadata. These capabilities are not current implementation or release prerequisites. Their retained IDs, historical decisions and examples are future traceability only and do not enable commands through direct/operator wrappers. Migration is excluded and import remains deferred.

**Additional scope reduction — user decision, 2026-10-15.** Defer trial extension and repeat trials, standalone acquisition without Orders, scheduled composition changes and scheduled resume, linked predecessor/successor subscription replacement, and changes of an existing subscription's base plan. Reject these operations before effects, including through operator/direct wrappers; earlier procedures and decisions for them are future traceability only. New paid and trial subscriptions originate through Orders. Existing-subscription lifecycle operations, supported immediate quantity increases/component additions, future order starts, cancellation scheduling, renewal and normal trial timers remain. Technical retry/successor attempts for the same acquisition remain recovery, not linked replacement of an existing subscription.

Free-period and credit trials, normal expiry/conversion and early conversion to the already accepted paid target remain in scope with their Pricing/Billing/financial gates. Trial-to-paid conversion is not a general changePlan capability. Preserve trial history/eligibility evidence and refuse repeat requests for the same payer/plan, including via a new subscription; a transport retry is not a repeat trial. No extension or repeat becomes available from a Pricing policy alone. Independent cancellation and new purchase have separate resource/data lifecycles: there is no automatic resource adoption, data transfer, place handover or continuity promise. Cancellation retains its existing retention/disposition rules; it does not mean immediate deletion. Unsupported plan changes cannot silently become cancel+new.

Keep ordinary acquisition/lifecycle, current scope authorization, tenant axes fixed for the subscription, immutable accepted terms, current commercial facts/full replacement versions, reliable operations and domain events/outbox. Ordinary retries, late authoritative outcomes and downstream Rating/Billing corrections remain part of their existing protocols; they do not create an administrative past-edit API. A dedicated audit store/query/export/projection is not required: domain events carry relevant actor, reason and correlation provenance for a later audit consumer, while operation receipts and financial/source history remain necessary for correctness. A deferred capability cannot be reported as fulfilled. If accepted terms require deferred acceptance, notice or other unavailable behavior, the affected profile remains unavailable; removing notice implementation does not silently waive a contractual prerequisite or authorize destructive cleanup without it. Basic opt-out still uses ordinary end-of-term cancellation, independently of a notification feature.

**External execution abstraction, 2026-10-14:** entitlement fulfillment, resource execution and inventory/evidence are abstract capability roles. Our LM/OSS, third-party services and customer integrations are alternative providers. S-U02/S-U04/S-U05/S-U09 describe applicable evidence and safety requirements, not mandatory APIs of those native systems. Vendor response/status/schema design is deferred to adapter specifications; core documentation does not wait for native LM/OSS PRDs. Only the selected product/deployment's required capabilities need demonstrated adapters before runtime enablement. The [provider boundary](../../entitlements/docs/DESIGN.md#11-architectural-vision), owned by Entitlements, preserves unknown-outcome, stale-action and historical-evidence guarantees.

**Subscriptions-owned recovery profile, 2026-10-14:** [feature 09 §3.8](features/09-consumer-contracts.md#38-recover-gaps-cursor-loss-and-retained-history) now specifies transactional commit-ordered journals, versioned snapshot membership and scoped continuation, commercial change catch-up for late corrections, checkpoint staging and typed bootstrap/history-loss recovery.
