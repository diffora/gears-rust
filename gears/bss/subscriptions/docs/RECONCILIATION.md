## 4. Decision package

The retained S-Q01-S-Q07 product decisions are accepted. Their current technical contracts and capability-specific delivery gates are in [section 6.1](#61-remaining-work-and-closure-order), not in the dated verification history below. [DECISIONS](DECISIONS.md#open-cross-owner-decisions) preserves the decisions and their later scope overrides.

The five completed semantic packages cover protected Orders activation (AP), external financial admission (FA), PlanRevision trials (TP), Subscriptions/Rating/Billing replacements and recovery (RB), and renewal/offboarding (RO). They specify operation identities, accepted evidence, local commit boundaries, competing outcomes and recovery without making provider availability or independent ratification claims. Exact versioned SDK/provider mappings and executed conformance evidence remain delivery work.

Current exclusions override earlier trial-exception, replacement, transfer and migration proposals. Migration is excluded; import, trial extension/repeat, linked subscription replacement, base-plan changes and the other deferred PRD capabilities add no implementation or launch gates. Change Orders authoring remains in its separate PRD. Entitlement/resource/inventory integrations use deployment-selected adapters; native LM/OSS designs are not core completion prerequisites. Required unavailable notice still blocks its dependent action despite deferred notification delivery.
