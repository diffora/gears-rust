# Acceptance and delivery gates

**Status: acceptance specification.** Each enabled deployment profile must record its SDK/provider versions and executed evidence for the applicable cases below.

## Delivery gates

| Gate | Accountable owners | Required closure evidence | Initial status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G01 Cross-gear contracts | Entitlements, QE, Subscriptions, Products, Pricing | Versioned SDK contracts implement the ownership, authority and data boundaries in DESIGN §3.1–3.4 | OPEN |
| G02 Catalog and terms | Products, types-registry, adapter owners, Pricing, Entitlements | Registered GTS schemas/instances and Products discovery SDK; schema/reference/authority validation, snapshot replay/conflict, exact conversions, localized metric presentation with fallback, reviewed metric/mapping publication, Products-owned qualification/availability and Pricing validation of the complete quota set, with frozen rule/receipt evidence | OPEN |
| G03 Purchase and quota readiness | Orders, Subscriptions, Entitlements | Accepted quota evidence and committed posture consumed through owner contracts; complete correlated quota confirmation without resource creation; allocation continuity across owner-controlled activation and lifecycle transitions | OPEN |
| G04 OSS admission and accounting | OSS/resource-service owners, adapter owner and authorization owner | Concurrent admission across all required metrics and aggregate tenant limits; no unaccounted resources or reused occupied capacity after failures; generation-specific, idempotent release evidence. Reservation and recovery mechanisms are covered by the OSS design | OPEN |
| G05 Quota application adapter | Adapter owner and Entitlements | Versioned application/read/recovery contract with pending/applied/refused/unknown semantics, authorized scope mapping, replay and ordering guarantees, correlated effective-limit evidence; OSS recipient and application mechanism covered by the adapter design | OPEN per adapter |
| G06 Consistent application | Entitlements and quota application adapter owner | One BSS operation for the complete set; incomplete/unknown outcomes and recovery; prior safe allowance retained during partial increases; OSS coordination covered by adapter conformance | OPEN per profile |
| G07 Runtime operations | Entitlements and adapter/service owners | Numeric profile bounds, restart/outage/overload tests, observations and freshness, durable failure handoff to the Entitlements recovery contract, source isolation and repair evidence | OPEN |
| G08 End-to-end offer | Products/Pricing, Orders, Subscriptions, Entitlements and provider owners | Recorded 10 vCPU / RAM plan journey with the provider's exact representable unit, all applicable cases below, and explicit refused-profile cases | OPEN |

An acceptance record contains owner approval/reference, producer and consumer versions, exact provider release, metric and subject mapping digests, capability profile/version, enabled/refused scenarios, fixture/run references and unresolved limitations. Each provider implementation and capability profile qualifies independently. Documentation agreement or a CRUD signature does not close a gate.

Consumption, pooling, arbitrary budget partitions, voluntary purchase decreases/base-plan changes, unbounded capacity, resource adoption and provider migration are outside the initial profile. Cases that cover consumption apply only when that profile is separately qualified. Monetary/credit-trial and named-grant profiles also require their respective financial and licensing contracts.

## Quota application and enforcement

These scenarios verify accepted terms, source-owned application, allocation continuity and enforcement. Runtime accounting, operational reads and admission are verified against OSS contracts; they do not require BSS to collect or expose usage counters. Lifecycle outcomes and resource readiness are inputs from the Entitlements and Subscriptions contracts; these cases do not redefine or test the full activation protocol. They specify required quota outcomes; execution results must be recorded per deployment profile.

| Case | Stimulus | Required observable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q-C01 Accepted evidence | Publish a new plan/metric revision after purchase | Replay retains the original rule/unit/quantity/digest; no live-catalog fallback. Missing history is pending/refused, not unlimited. |
| Q-C02 Numeric limits | Fractional integer result, overflow, negative value or unit mismatch | Refuse before effects with a specific reason; exact valid result accepted. Test zero separately from absent/unbounded. |
| Q-C03 Quantity basis | Plan quantity three; several item selections | Per-plan-unit rule applied once with quantity three; fixed rule once. No per-component quantity is invented. |
| Q-C04 Quota readiness | Buy a 100 GiB allocation limit in an empty scope; delay or mismatch the adapter confirmation. Separately provide an authorized prepared 20 GiB allocation from the resource fulfillment contract | Complete applied-and-available confirmation establishes the quota obligation without creating a resource. Partial, unknown or mismatched evidence does not. Prepared occupancy is 20 GiB with 80 GiB available and is preserved across activation. Resource readiness is a separate obligation; the general activation protocol is verified by its owners. A draft alone cannot authorize a grant. |
| Q-C05 Purchase replay | Same purchase with another transport key | Same lot and accepted snapshot recovered; no extra quantity or quota. Changed purchase content conflicts. |
| Q-C06 Compatible increase | Original quantity one at 10 vCPU per plan unit, then add one compatible unit; separately use Fixed(10 vCPU) | Subscriptions supplies effective quantity two; Entitlements replaces the same source cap with 20 for PerPlanUnit and retains 10 for Fixed. Original receipt still records one, both purchases remain provenance and occupancy is unchanged. Replay creates no extra allowance. Unsupported delta mapping or different-rule combination refuses. |
| Q-C07 Suspended purchase and race | Request an increase/addition while suspended, or suspend before its commit; separately delay an already committed active increase | Refuse uncommitted purchases with no quantity/lot/fact/provider effects or queued work. Resume is a separate guarded operation. Preserve previously committed purchase history, and fence its delayed delivery behind the newer restriction. |
| Q-C08 Unknown apply | Provider succeeds, reply lost; worker restarts | Source read/replay returns same applied revision/digest and one effect; unknown is visible until resolved. |
| Q-C09 Accepted revision fence and refusal replay | Start at applied revision 5. Accept revision 7, then refuse it; delay unseen revision 6 and superseded in-flight work, restart, and replay revision 7. Try malformed/unauthorized higher requests, then an authorized corrected revision 8. Separately close the source before a delayed grant | Accepted head remains 7 while applied stays 5. Old work cannot newly apply; known history and unresolved effects remain recoverable. Replay 7 returns its refusal, with no fresh execution. Invalid/unauthorized/conflicting requests do not advance the fence. Corrected 8 is a new operation under the same commercial provenance. Terminal closure rejects all later grants; no lost response, retry or restart reopens the source. |
| Q-C10 Ownership conflict | Another source or operator record matches subject/metric | No overwrite, deletion, implicit merge or double allowance. Atomic ownership admission refuses or follows an explicitly adopted policy. |
| Q-C11 Tenant isolation | Seller/payer tenant or arbitrary foreign resource supplied as target | Refuse without a resource-owner binding proof; authorized recovery cannot expose another tenant's state. |
| Q-C12 Allocation lifecycle continuity | A 20-vCPU allocation quota has 8 vCPU occupied. Renew the subscription, suspend/resume under owner-authorized posture, and duplicate/reorder delivery. Separately exercise a consumption window only when that profile is qualified | Allocation source/member/resource identities and 8 occupied vCPU remain unchanged across renewal and suspension/resume; no refill, duplicate allowance or implicit release. Admission follows the current authorized posture; after confirmed resume the unchanged 20-vCPU cap leaves 12 available absent another restriction. For separately qualified consumption, window identity and usage are preserved until the supported owner-defined window transition. |
| Q-C13 Expiry geometry | Request at exact exclusive expiry; delayed timer | Access follows `[start,end)` without endpoint overlap. Unsupported inclusive-only mapping prevents enabling timed profiles. |
| Q-C14 Mixed grants | Different windows, units, expiries or incompatible terms | Refuse the unsupported profile before sale/change; no accidental sum/max, new counter or silent extension. |
| Q-C15 Below used | Applicable cap falls below occupied/spent amount | Preserve evidence; block further positive admission according to the adopted policy; no silent eviction, refund or usage clamp. |
| Q-C16 Revocation fallback | Specific quota revoked while parent/default policy permits | Required access remains denied by the accepted restriction policy; mere deactivation is not enough. |
| Q-C17 Atomic set | Failure halfway through a multi-member desired update | Atomic apply or safe restricted pending state with recovery; unsupported multi-member profiles refused. No falsely complete partial grant. |
| Q-C18 Runtime capability | Required primitive absent, or an enabled provider temporarily unavailable | Absent capability blocks enabling the profile. A transient outage leaves delivery pending/unknown under the accepted acquisition contract; no fabricated applied result or direct DB workaround. |
| Q-C19 Counter identity change | Subject/metric/kind/period change presented as update | Refuse unsupported migration; do not mint a fresh allowance under a new record. |
| Q-C20 Financial separation | Change monetary minimum or exhaust trial credit | No inferred quota arithmetic. Financial/lifecycle owners determine posture; hard access behavior still needs its own provider evidence. |
| Q-C21 Metric drift | A metric becomes Direct/inert or loses registration after materialization | Read/report materialization separately from enforcement; failed updates retain the purchased obligation and surface owner remediation. No silent metric substitution or false readiness. |
| Q-C22 Binding equivalence | A newer PlanRevision reuses an item ID with changed quota semantics | Do not treat it as the same quota binding. Require the agreed semantic-equivalence contract and all distinct-binding gates before combining lots. |
| Q-C23 Preparation-to-service continuity | Supply authorized preparation followed by an applied activation outcome through owner contracts; delay or replay lifecycle delivery while allocations are in flight | Preserve source/member/resource/allocation identities and occupied/reserved amounts. No second allowance, reset or revoke/recreate gap. Activation timing and outcome recovery remain owner-contract inputs. |
| Q-C24 Aborted preparation occupancy | Supply an authorized withdrawal through the lifecycle contract; resource release is delayed or an in-flight allocation completes late | Apply the authorized quota restriction, retain counted capacity while effects remain unresolved, and settle only confirmed release once. A withdrawal, timer or dispatched delete alone is not release proof. |
| Q-C25 Unknown lifecycle outcome | The owner contract cannot yet supply an authoritative activation or withdrawal outcome, then supplies one | Preserve quota and allocation identities through uncertainty. Consume the resolved current posture without counter deletion, duplicate allowance or historical regrant. The quota workflow does not decide activation or cancellation. |
| Q-C26 Readiness evidence and accounting | An adapter produces quota readiness evidence using its supported validation mechanism; that mechanism may have real allocation effects | Validation alone grants no extra allowance or accounting exemption. Any actual allocation remains correlated and counted under the purchased limit. Replay preserves counters. A check marker alone cannot establish quota readiness. |
| Q-C27 Purchased limit and fulfillment discrepancy | The adapter reports that a purchased 100 GiB cap is blocked by a 60 GiB ceiling; separately OSS accounts for 40 GiB used under a fully applied 100 GiB cap | BSS preserves purchased 100 and exposes the first case as an adapter-reported fulfillment discrepancy with reason/evidence; no required quota readiness. Ordinary OSS consumption creates no discrepancy or change to the purchased cap. BSS needs no usage-counter projection to distinguish the outcomes. |
| Q-C28 Fulfillment discrepancy recovery | An OSS restriction prevents fulfillment without changing commercial posture; the adapter discrepancy observation is lost or reordered, then the adapter confirms recovery | BSS retains terms and the unresolved discrepancy, recovers the ordered adapter result and clears it only from correlated recovery evidence. OSS retains authority over its restrictions and counters; BSS does not repair them directly. |
| Q-C29 Restriction after quota readiness | An independent effective ceiling or security restriction changes after quota confirmation, including during owner-controlled activation | The adapter reports any resulting fulfillment discrepancy to Entitlements; BSS preserves desired terms and OSS preserves occupancy. Prior quota confirmation cannot hide the restriction. Activation arbitration remains with the lifecycle owners; quota handling creates no refund, refill, counter clamp or eviction. |

## Allocation release and accounting

Resource lifecycle commands and cleanup are exercised by their owner contracts. These cases verify how quota accounting consumes their release evidence; a dispatched stop/delete is not confirmed release.

| Case | Situation | Required behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q-C30 Stop and per-metric occupancy | A billing cutoff or stop request occurs before release is confirmed; a qualified profile later confirms CPU/RAM release while storage remains retained | Cutoff/request alone returns no capacity. Settle only the confirmed CPU/RAM amounts at their actual release outcome; keep storage counted. Quota accounting does not decide charging or resource stop policy. |
| Q-C31 Duplicate release evidence | Deliver the same confirmed release repeatedly and concurrently; restart accounting after settlement but before the response | One settlement for the original allocation and release identity. Recovery returns its recorded result; occupied capacity is reduced once and the purchased cap stays unchanged. |
| Q-C32 Reused resource name | Allocation A has release evidence delayed until another resource generation B uses the same name | Evidence settles only A in its original acquisition. B retains its occupancy; no name-based lookup credits the new allocation or reopens the old grant. |
| Q-C33 Partial or unknown release | A 20 GiB allocation has only 8 GiB confirmed released; the remaining outcome is unknown. Replay the 8 GiB evidence, then confirm a distinct remaining 12 GiB release | Initially return exactly 8 GiB and retain 12 GiB occupied. Replay returns nothing extra. The distinct remaining release settles once when confirmed; no full release is inferred from a partial result. |
| Q-C34 Conflicting or unauthorized evidence | Replay one release identity with a changed amount; submit evidence for a foreign tenant, wrong allocation, wrong unit or more than the remaining accounted amount | Refuse the invalid settlement without counter changes and expose the reason for recovery. No release claim grants arbitrary credit or affects another allocation. |
| Q-C35 Prepared allocation release unknown | Authorized cleanup is initiated through the resource fulfillment contract, but release is partial or unknown | Retain unresolved occupied capacity and allocation identity until authoritative generation-specific release evidence arrives; settle confirmed release once. Cleanup orchestration remains with the resource fulfillment contract. |
| Q-C36 Release after newer posture | Release evidence for an original allocation arrives after a restriction, revocation or new purchase; some original effects remain unknown | Settle confirmed release against the original allocation once, retain unknown occupancy and preserve the current restriction. Neither late evidence nor settlement revives an old grant or credits a new acquisition. |

| Q-C37 GTS catalog intake | Discover a valid typed capability snapshot; replay it, change content at the same revision, then offer unknown/wrong-family references or another scope. Repeat through manual authoring and open the plan form in a supported and an untranslated locale; try a metric without required display text | Products stages only authorized schema-validated candidates with pinned metric/unit references. Replay is idempotent; conflicting revision, wrong scope or invalid references refuse. Review and qualification precede plan publication. No automatic purchase or tenant mutation; manual input has the same gates. The plan form shows reviewed title/description, uses English fallback and shows the unit and selected profile conditions; missing required text blocks publication. Numeric bounds are converted exactly and never predefine the purchased quantity |
| Q-C38 Units, versions and discovery continuity | Import allocation, feature, monetary and retention settings; test GB/GiB conversion, unsupported step, zero denominator, changed same-ID semantics, mixed snapshot pages, registry outage and later capability retirement | Only qualified allocation records enter this profile. Conversion is exact with registered immutable units; invalid arithmetic/versions/conflicts refuse. Incomplete snapshots and registry outages cannot authorize publication or infer removal. New definitions require review; accepted references and limits remain unchanged |
| Q-C39 Plan packaging | Same SKU in plans selling 10 and 20 vCPU; quantity two; Fixed(10 resource instances); repeated item selection | Correct distinct accepted caps; per-unit multiplication once; fixed once; no duplicate binding effect. CPU-only add-on and voluntary decrease refuse when unsupported |
| Q-C40 Commercial protection | Future purchase, catalog retirement, expired assessment, lost draft and lost capture reply | Original terms/claim/financial evidence survive. | Q-C41 Application result contract | Apply only part of the required set; lose an internal outcome; exhaust retries; recover. Also reject a no-effect stale request and send a conflicting replay for an already recorded operation | Known partial application is pending, uncertainty is unknown, and neither becomes refused from retry exhaustion. Applied requires the complete correlated change. Refused requires a definitive reason and no unresolved effects. Recovery uses the same identity; conflicting replay cannot overwrite the original result. Old observations cannot erase a terminal result or complete a newer revision; no partial result establishes quota readiness |
| Q-C42 Partial increase | Increase 10 to 20; the adapter reports partial application while allocations continue | The adapter retains the previous safe allowance of 10 until the complete increase is applied. BSS shows the increase as incomplete and recovers the same operation; occupied capacity is unchanged |
| Q-C43 Restriction and stale completion | Suspend/revoke while an older grant/resume is in flight; restart BSS and adapter workers | The adapter prevents older work from reopening access. BSS cannot complete the newer revision from an old result; unknown effects remain recoverable |
| Q-C44 Shared users | Two users concurrently allocate in the same purchased tenant; a user-specific quota also exists | Combined allocations never exceed the tenant cap. User policy cannot displace it. Wrong-owner principal mapping refuses |

| Q-C46 Multi-metric admission and failure | Request a VM when CPU quota is sufficient but RAM is insufficient; race two requests whose combined demand exceeds a required limit. Separately interrupt an otherwise admissible create and delay its outcome | OSS admits no VM without sufficient quota for every required metric. Concurrent requests cannot exceed the applicable limits. Interrupted or late effects remain accounted for and recoverable under their original identities; potentially occupied capacity is not reused and no outcome is accounted twice. Verify through OSS contracts without prescribing reservation or compensation internals |
| Q-C47 Allocation lifecycle evidence | Consume create, resize, stop and release outcomes from the resource owner contract, including duplicates and lost responses | Account each confirmed allocation/release effect once under its immutable generation and qualified per-metric semantics. Unknown outcomes remain recoverable; no double debit, duplicate return or settlement against a replacement generation. |
| Q-C48 Authorization | Authorized seller/payer submits a foreign resource scope; service credential reads another namespace's result | No mutation or protected-state disclosure; target owner and delegated source authority required on every operation |
| Q-C49 Reduction below use | Provider/lifecycle restriction below occupied amount; customer requests voluntary downgrade | Preserve occupied evidence, block new disallowed admission, no eviction/refill/refund. Voluntary downgrade refuses as a deferred commercial feature |
| Q-C50 OSS accounting ownership | Several internal OSS systems account for the same allocation; their observations disagree. Query BSS and OSS read contracts | OSS reconciles its accounting and owns operational usage/remaining reads. BSS exposes purchased limits and application state without collecting, summing or persisting an operational usage projection. If the inconsistency prevents fulfillment, the adapter reports a correlated discrepancy to BSS. |
| Q-C51 Routing and revision replay | Change adapter selection during unknown operation; old callback arrives after newer desired head | Recover via saved adapter/provider binding. No fallback grant, no new-head completion from old reply; unresolved old effects retained |
| Q-C52 BSS application status and freshness | An adapter reports a restriction preventing fulfillment, then evidence becomes stale, is lost or arrives out of order; the portal also requests operational quota values | BSS returns purchased limits, application status, evidence time and unresolved discrepancy. Stale or missing evidence cannot clear the discrepancy or imply success. Operational effective limits, used, reserved and remaining amounts come from OSS contracts, not Entitlements. |
| Q-C53 Provider bypass/adoption | Attempt positive allocation through direct console/API; start with existing unaccounted resources | Strict profile mediates/denies uncontrolled path. Nonempty scope refuses initial profile; no QE zero-usage bootstrap or claim that eventual import provides strict admission |
| Q-C54 Qualification, availability and overload | Discovery declares support without conformance evidence; CPU and RAM are qualified separately but not jointly; an adapter version changes; qualification is disabled or withdrawn after publication; a qualified adapter becomes unavailable | Products enables only matching adapter-owner qualification. Pricing rejects unsupported complete sets and rechecks eligibility at acceptance; stale publication evidence cannot authorize a new sale. Disabled/withdrawn qualification blocks new sales but preserves accepted terms and fulfillment obligations. Isolated qualification testing is possible before sale enablement. Physical capacity remains an OSS check. Outages/retry exhaustion preserve the obligation, original identities and latest outcome/reason, with a durable handoff to Entitlements recovery that survives restart. A definitive adapter refusal also leaves the commercial obligation unresolved. Handoff/acknowledgment does not mark the operation applied, cancel a purchase, refund money or release occupied capacity; no fallback is success |
| Q-C55 Quota and charging independence | Accept package, usage and mixed plans with the same 20-vCPU ceiling; allocate 4 vCPU or allocate nothing. Exhaust an included billing-usage allowance, and increase subscription quantity under Fixed and PerPlanUnit rules | All three apply the same allocation ceiling and account 4 occupied vCPU identically. Accepted terms separately identify package charges, usage-meter/rate references and inclusions; owner billing fixtures verify included versus chargeable usage without inferring it from the cap. No usage produces no charge for a purely usage-priced component. An exhausted inclusion does not raise the cap or authorize bypass; restrictions require owner posture. Quantity scaling follows the quota rule, independent of the charging model. No paid flag or tariff enters the quota adapter request. |
| Q-C56 Closed-source recovery | Close a source with occupied capacity and an unknown operation; attempt a higher-revision grant/increase/resume, recover a historical applied grant, submit a denial-preserving update and confirmed partial release, then start a new purchase in a conflicting scope | Closed source rejects all regrant attempts with source_closed. Authorized reads/recovery and valid denial-preserving updates remain available; recovery does not reexecute the historical grant. Confirmed release settles only the original allocation once without restoring rights; unknown occupancy stays tracked. The new purchase has a distinct source, inherits no allowance or allocations, and cannot bypass scope conflicts. Temporary suspension remains resumable under its owner-authorized posture. |

## Verification method

Run BSS scenarios against the versioned adapter contract, including authorization, duplicate delivery, process death, response loss, incomplete outcomes and reordered revisions. Adapter conformance separately proves that these results reflect actual OSS effects under partial application and concurrent resource operations. A dispatch receipt alone cannot prove completion.

Record each run with the tested profile/version, environment, fixture, observed outcome and evidence reference. A passing state-store test does not replace adapter and provider fault testing.
